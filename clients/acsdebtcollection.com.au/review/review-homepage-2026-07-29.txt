PART 3 — ISSUES FOUND ON THE HOMEPAGE
================================================================================

H1. COMPANY AGE CONTRADICTS ITSELF ON THE SAME PAGE          ** fix first **
    Three different statements are live simultaneously:
      Body copy   : "a specialist debt collection agency with 29 YEARS of
                     industry experience"          <- from the approved doc
      Stats tile  : "Over 30 YEARS In The Industry" <- the screenshot said KEEP
      Footer      : "ACS Debt Collection Celebrating 29 YEARS"
    A visitor scrolling the page sees 29, then 30, then 29.

    IMPORTANT — this is NOT a developer error. Annotation 5 on the screenshot
    explicitly said "Keep these" over the stats block containing the 30-years
    tile, while the approved copy written for the same page says 29 years. The
    two instructions conflict and nobody caught it before deployment. The
    developer followed both correctly.

    ACTION: decide the correct figure and apply it in all three places. If it is
    30, the approved document needs correcting too; if it is 29, the stats tile
    needs editing despite the "keep" instruction.

H2. NO FAQPage SCHEMA — homepage and sitewide         ** biggest SEO gap **
    The homepage has 8 FAQs correctly marked up as H3 with answers below, but
    emits no FAQPage / Question / Answer structured data. Schema present is
    only: WebPage, WebSite, Organization, BreadcrumbList, ImageObject,
    SearchAction, ReadAction, EntryPoint, ListItem, QuantitativeValue.
    This is not homepage-specific. Checked all 11 pages in this project:

      PAGE                                    FAQs on page   FAQPage schema
      /                                             8              NO
      /debt-collection-melbourne/                  10              NO
      /debt-collection-sydney/                     10              NO
      /debt-collection-brisbane/                    9              NO
      /commercial-debt-collection-services/         9              NO
      /collection-agency-small-business/            8              NO
      /outsource-debt-collection/                   8              NO
      /commission-only-debt-collection/             7              NO
      /skip-tracing/                                8              NO
      /repossession-agents-melbourne/               8              NO
      /mortgagee-in-possession/                     9              NO
                                                  ---
                                                   94 FAQs, zero schema

    94 FAQs were written, approved and deployed, and none of them are eligible
    for FAQ rich results or easily extractable for AI answer engines.
    ACTION: add FAQPage JSON-LD. Template-level fix, applies to all 11 pages.

H3. EMPTY H2 IN THE MARKUP
    The page contains a literal `<h2 class=""></h2>` with no content. Harmless
    to users, but it is an empty heading in the document outline.
    ACTION: remove.

RESOLVED — "legacy promo sections" are NOT an issue
    An earlier pass flagged three sections as possibly-unremoved legacy content
