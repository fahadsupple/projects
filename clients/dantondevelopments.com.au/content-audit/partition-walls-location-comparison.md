Live: `Compliances and management approvals`  
Staging: `Building permits and management approvals`  
Confirm which version is correct. If staging is intentional, no action needed. If live is correct, revert on staging.

**3. Process sections lose numbered format on Preston and Grovedale**  
"The Danton Process" (Preston) and "The Danton Developments Installation Process" (Grovedale) both change from numbered paragraphs (e.g., `1. **Initial Consultation** – ...`) to a plain bullet list on staging. Step numbers are removed. If a numbered process flow was intentional for those pages, the developer needs to restore it.

**4. Williams Landing — Partial typo fix**  
Step 1 "1Initial Consultation" typo is fixed on staging. Step 2 "Design and Quot" is not. Both should be reviewed together.

**5. "Why Choose" / CTA / process sections repositioned on all pages**  
On staging, these sections consistently move relative to FAQ (some before, some after). The pattern is consistent across all 13 pages, indicating intentional template restructuring. Confirm this is the desired reading flow before launch.
