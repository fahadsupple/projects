## Content Strategy Insight
- **thedetailmarket.com.au** has a Shopify /collections/car-washing-supplies-melbourne — this is exactly the URL model for eazygleam's city pages
- Competitors like gleamitproducts.com.au and carcareproducts.com.au already have city-specific landing pages
- eazygleam should create /pages/[city]-car-detailing-products or /collections/[city] pages to target these keywords
