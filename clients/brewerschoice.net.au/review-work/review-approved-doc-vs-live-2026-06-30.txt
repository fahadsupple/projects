ISSUE 9: NON-SELF-REFERENCING CANONICAL — TOP SHELF ESSENCE PAGE
URL: https://brewerschoice.net.au/product-category/spirit-making/flavouring-essences-chips/top-shelf-essence/

  Page is served at:
    .../flavouring-essences-chips/top-shelf-essence/

  Canonical tag points to:
    .../flavouring-essences-chips/flavouring-chips/top-shelf-essence/

  These are two different URLs. The canonical includes an extra path segment
  "flavouring-chips/" that is not in the actual page URL. This is a broken
  canonical — Google will attempt to use the canonical URL as the primary
  index target, but that URL may not match the page's actual location.

  Note: The approved document lists the URL without the trailing slash or the
  "flavouring-chips/" segment — the original approved URL was:
    .../flavouring-essences-chips/top-shelf-essence

  It is likely this category was nested under a parent "flavouring-chips"
  subcategory at some point, and the canonical was not updated when the URL
  structure changed (or vice versa).

Action needed: Developer to investigate the WooCommerce category hierarchy for
"Top Shelf Essence" and ensure the canonical URL matches the actual served URL.
The canonical should be self-referencing.

VERDICT
================================================================================

The meta layer (titles, descriptions, OG tags) is solid — all 26 match exactly.
The main risk areas are:

1. WooCommerce H1 overrides — 6 product category pages show the internal
   WooCommerce category label as the H1 instead of the approved SEO heading.
   This is the most common WooCommerce implementation issue and is the highest
   SEO priority to fix.

2. FAQ/CTA section order — 7 product category pages have the closing sections
   in the wrong order. Minor SEO impact but means the live content doesn't
   match the approved document.

3. Broken canonical (Top Shelf Essence) — needs to be corrected before the
   page can be indexed correctly.

Of the 16 action items: 1 is CRITICAL, 6 are HIGH, 8 are MEDIUM, 1 is LOW.
Fix the CRITICAL and HIGH items first (items 1-7). Items 1 and 5-7 may all
resolve through a single WooCommerce H1 configuration change if the developer
decouples the category name from the page H1.

Once items 1-7 are resolved, the site will be structurally consistent with
the approved document. Items 8-16 can follow in the next pass.
