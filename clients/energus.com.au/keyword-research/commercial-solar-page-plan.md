### On canonicals

Don't use canonicals here. Canonical tags are appropriate when a page needs to remain accessible (e.g. faceted navigation, paginated results) but you want to signal the master. In this case:
- `/solar-energy-for-aussie-business/` should be deleted and 301'd — not canonicalled
- `/commercial-solar-panels/` and `/commercial-solar-installer/` should be kept with unique content — not canonicalled to this page
- If you can't yet rewrite those two pages, a temporary canonical from `/commercial-solar-panels/` → `/commercial-solar-installer/` would resolve the duplicate between those two, but is not a substitute for writing unique content
