## KEY RESEARCH FINDINGS (GKP AU, 2026-07-14)

### 1. Root keyword test — gerund beats plural noun decisively
| Root | Suburbs w/ volume (of 20) | Competition | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`house cleaning [suburb]`** | **17** | mostly LOW/MED | ✅ **THE ROOT** |
| `house cleaners [suburb]` | **4** (Brighton 90, Cheltenham 50, Carnegie 20, Caulfield 20) | LOW/MED | ❌ no coverage |
| `cleaning services [suburb]` | 17 | mostly HIGH | ❌ office/commercial ambiguity |
| `cleaners [suburb]` (existing) | 18 | mostly HIGH | secondary only |

`house cleaning [suburb]` volumes: Brighton 110, Cheltenham 50, Caulfield 20, Carnegie 20, rest 10.
N/A: Gardenvale, McKinnon, Ormond.
Same volume as `cleaners` but **LOW/MED comp vs HIGH** — softer SERP, cleaner residential intent.

### 2. `cleaners [suburb]` and `house cleaning [suburb]` are the SAME SERP
SERP-checked Brighton: both return Maid To Clean, MyHome, Maid2Match, Crystal Maids, Airtasker — same
competitors, same page type. **One intent → pair as P + S on ONE page, never two pages.**
Non-subset rule passes ("cleaners" not contained in "house cleaning brighton").

### 3. NO viable second location root exists
| Candidate | Suburbs w/ volume | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `deep cleaning [suburb]` | 2/20 | dead |
| `domestic cleaning [suburb]` | 7/20 @ 10/mo HIGH | too weak |
| `airbnb cleaning [suburb]` | 1/20 | dead |
| `carpet` / `window cleaning [suburb]` | real vol (Brighton 210 ea) | client doesn't offer |

→ Freed slots must go to **general keywords**, not a second suburb root.

### 4. "Large / luxury home" keywords DO NOT EXIST — tested exhaustively
**All returned N/A from GKP:** `luxury house cleaning melbourne`, `mansion cleaning melbourne`,
`large house cleaning`, `large home cleaning`, `premium cleaning melbourne`, `high end cleaning melbourne`,
`double storey house cleaning`, `two storey house cleaning`, `4 bedroom house cleaning`,
`5 bedroom house cleaning`, `3 bedroom house cleaning`, `house cleaning cost 4 bedroom`,
`cleaning subscription melbourne`, `cleaning membership melbourne`.
Luxury lexicon is near-dead too: `white glove cleaning` 40, `estate cleaning` 20, `concierge cleaning` 10.

**Rule learned:** you cannot target house SIZE or LUXURY through keywords. It is won through
(a) suburb selection (big-house suburbs) and (b) page content/pricing qualifying the lead.
Only route to the large-house searcher is informational: `how much to clean a 4 bedroom house` (10),
`how long to clean a 4 bedroom house` (10) — blog/AEO content, not commercial targets.

### 5. `housekeeper melbourne` (210) — REJECTED, job-listing SERP
SERP = Jora, Glassdoor, Care.com jobs, Elite Private Staff recruitment. Job-seeker intent, not buyer.
Classic mixed-SERP reject.
