**What should a cleaner do in 2 hours?**
In a standard visit, our pair works systematically through the living areas, kitchen and bathrooms: dusting reachable surfaces, wiping benches and cupboard exteriors, cleaning the stovetop and appliance fronts, scrubbing and disinfecting bathrooms and toilets, polishing tapware and mirrors, then vacuuming and mopping the floors. Two people covering this together get far more done in the time than one.

**What will a cleaner do in 3 hours?**
A longer visit or a deep clean adds the detail beyond a standard tidy: scrubbing shower and wall grout, cleaning inside the oven, dishwasher and fridge, wiping skirting boards and window sills and tracks, lightly dusting blinds, and refreshing soft furnishings. We tailor the scope to your home so the time is spent where it matters most.

**What do housekeepers not clean?**
Our standard cleans focus on the accessible, everyday surfaces that keep a home fresh, so a few things sit outside them by default: the inside of ovens, fridges and dishwashers, interior windows and high or hard-to-reach areas, and heavy build-up like extensive grout scrubbing. Most of these are included in a deep clean or can be added on request, so nothing is off the table, it is simply matched to the service you book.

**What is the 20 minute rule of cleaning?**
The "20 minute rule" is a popular habit of spending a focused 20 minutes a day tidying to stop mess accumulating. It helps day to day, but it will not replace a thorough, systematic clean. That is exactly what our dedicated team delivers, so your own time is freed for the things that matter more than housework.
