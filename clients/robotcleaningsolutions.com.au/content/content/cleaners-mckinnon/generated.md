Advertised rates across Australia swing with the provider, the home and what counts as included, which is why McKinnon listings produce such a spread. We price the property rather than the clock, so the figure is agreed before the first visit and does not move.

**If I booked two hours of cleaning, what would realistically get done?**
With a pair on site, two hours is closer to four hours of one person's work. In an average McKinnon home that covers kitchen surfaces, bathrooms, floors and general dusting. Oven interiors, inside the fridge and shower grout belong to a deep clean, booked as its own visit.

**Everyone mentions the 20 minute rule. Is it worth doing in a family home?**
Giving one zone twenty focused minutes is a useful habit, and with children in the house it stops clutter compounding between visits. It maintains a home rather than resetting one, so it works alongside a professional clean, not in place of it.
