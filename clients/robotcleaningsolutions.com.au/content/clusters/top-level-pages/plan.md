## Per-entry unique angle
### homepage
The brand front door. Leads with 'professional house cleaning company melbourne' and positions Robot Cleaning Solutions as the systematised, human, local alternative to the faceless national brands and gig marketplaces above/around it in the SERP. Its three sharpest hooks, in order: (1) the 'Robot Standard' two-person method (twice as fast, same team every visit); (2) the productised '24/7 Clean' membership no top-10 competitor offers; (3) transparent, tailored pricing answering the cost-dominated SERP demand. Carries the full whole-business trust stack and routes visitors onward to the two service hubs, the house-cleaning services overview, and the areas-we-serve directory. Note: the local pack and 1,000-3,000-review incumbents mean on-page cannot close the visibility gap alone — the page should convert the searchers it does reach, hard.

### house-clean
The house-cleaning services OVERVIEW hub for 'house cleaning services melbourne'. Presents a complete, scannable menu of the client's actual services — Weekly Residential, Fortnightly Residential, Deep Clean, One-Off Standard, End of Lease / Bond Back, Move-In, Corporate/Office, and Tile & Grout — each with a short 'what's included' so the page earns the expectation-setting trust signal Inner City Maids wins on, but around the client's REAL service names. Owns the Bayside/South-East niche the national brands ignore, fronted by named founder Zoe. Links each recurring service to its deeper hub (/regular-house-cleaning/, /luxury-house-cleaning/) and out to suburb pages.

### areas-we-serve
The service-area DIRECTORY for 'house cleaners near me'. Its job is to resolve the searcher's location for them: a scannable, grouped directory of all 20 Bayside/South-East suburbs (with postcodes) linking straight to each suburb page — a structural role NO ranking competitor fills. Counters the Airtasker/gig 'pot-luck cleaner' SERP with the same vetted, insured two-person Robot Standard team in every suburb, and anchors hub-level trust (vetted, insured, satisfaction-backed, fixed-price membership) once so every linked suburb page inherits the authority.
