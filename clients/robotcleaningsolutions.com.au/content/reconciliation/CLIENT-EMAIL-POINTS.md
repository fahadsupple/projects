# Client email — open questions on service details

**Context for us (not for the email):** all four questions below come from the same root cause. The information in the intake forms and the information published on the live website **disagree with each other** about what the service includes. We have written the content to the safest reading in each case, but each needs one line from the client to settle it. Two of these are on all 25 pages, so getting them right matters.

---

## Draft email

Hi Zoe,

As we finalised the new content we cross-checked every service detail against both your intake forms and your current website. Almost everything matched, but we found a few points where the two sources say different things. Could you confirm the following so we publish the right version?

**1. Emergency cleans: is it one per year, or two?**
Your form says the 24/7 Clean membership includes **1 yearly emergency clean**, but your current website lists **2 yearly emergency cleans**. We have written **one** across the new pages. If it is actually two, that is a better member benefit than we are currently advertising and we will update it everywhere.

**2. Is the membership available fortnightly as well as weekly?**
Your website's membership inclusion list says "Weekly cleaning session", while your form refers to weekly or fortnightly sessions and prices membership per week/fortnight. We have written **weekly or fortnightly**. Please confirm that is right.

**3. Do you offer monthly cleaning, and can it be a membership?**
Your Beaumaris page says you offer "flexible weekly, fortnightly, or monthly services", but monthly does not appear in the service list you gave us. We have added monthly to the regular cleaning page as an available rhythm, arranged and quoted at the on-site assessment, while keeping the 24/7 Clean membership described as weekly or fortnightly. Two things to confirm:
   a. Do you offer monthly cleaning? (We have assumed yes, based on your website.)
   b. Can monthly be taken as a membership, or is it arranged as a regular booking outside the membership?

**4. A note on your current website's pricing**
Following your instruction, we have removed all pricing from the new content. Your existing live pages still display the membership price tiers and the End of Lease starting price, so those blocks will need to come down at the same time the new content goes live, otherwise the old prices will still be visible on the site. Just flagging so nothing gets missed.

Happy to jump on a quick call if that is easier than replying point by point.

Thanks,
[name]
