# Client email — open questions on service details

**Context for us (not for the email):** all four points below share one root cause. The information in the intake forms and the information published on the live website **disagree with each other** about what the service includes. We have written the content to the safest reading in each case, but each needs one line from the client to settle it. Two of these appear on all 25 pages, so getting them right matters.

---

## Draft email

**Subject: Two quick service questions before we finalise your new website content**

Hi Zoe,

Your new content is ready. Before we hand it over, we cross-checked every service detail in it against two sources: the intake forms you filled in for us, and the copy currently published on your website.

Almost everything matched. But we found a few places where those two sources say different things about your service, and we did not want to guess. Could you confirm the four points below? Most are a one word answer.

---

### 1. Emergency cleans: is it one per year, or two?

**What we found.** The forms you completed tell us the 24/7 Clean membership includes **"1 yearly emergency clean"**. You mentioned it four separate times, so it is clearly deliberate. However, your current website says something different. The "What's Included In The Membership?" list on your homepage reads **"2 yearly emergency cleans"**.

**What we have published.** We went with **one** per year, and it appears on all 25 new pages.

**Why this one matters most.** If the real answer is two, then we are currently advertising a *smaller* benefit than you actually deliver, across your entire website. Emergency cleans are one of the strongest reasons someone joins a membership rather than booking ad hoc, so under-stating it costs you sign-ups. Conversely, if the real answer is one, your current website has been over-promising and that is worth correcting before someone claims a second clean.

**What we need from you:** just "one" or "two". If it is two, we will update every page.

---

### 2. Can the membership be fortnightly, or is it weekly only?

**What we found.** Your website's membership inclusion list says simply **"Weekly cleaning session"**, which reads as weekly only. Your forms suggest otherwise: they describe your team continuing **"weekly or fortnightly sessions"**, and your membership pricing was given to us on a **per week/fortnight** basis.

**What we have published.** We have described the membership as **weekly or fortnightly** throughout, since two of your own sources support it.

**Why it matters.** Fortnightly is the more affordable entry point and, in our experience, the rhythm a lot of households choose first. If it is genuinely available, it should be visible. If it is not available and we advertise it, you will field enquiries for something you do not offer.

**What we need from you:** confirm that fortnightly membership is available. Yes or no.

---

### 3. Do you offer monthly cleaning, and can it be a membership?

**What we found.** Monthly cleaning does not appear anywhere in the forms you completed. The service list you gave us contains a Weekly Residential Clean and a Fortnightly Residential Clean, but no monthly option. Your website says otherwise: your Beaumaris page answers a scheduling question with **"we offer flexible weekly, fortnightly, or monthly services."**

**What we have published.** Because your own website advertises it, we have included monthly on the regular house cleaning page as an available rhythm, described as something we arrange and quote at the free on-site assessment. We have deliberately kept the 24/7 Clean membership itself described as weekly or fortnightly, so we are not advertising a monthly membership tier you may not run.

**Why it matters.** "Monthly house cleaning Melbourne" is one of the keywords targeting that page, so the page needs to speak to it. But we will not advertise a service you do not actually provide, and if you do provide it, we would rather describe it accurately than vaguely.

**What we need from you, two parts:**
   **a.** Do you offer monthly cleaning at all? (We have assumed yes, based on your website.)
   **b.** If yes, can a customer take monthly *as a membership*, or is monthly arranged as a regular booking outside the 24/7 Clean membership?

---

### 4. A heads-up about pricing on your current website

You asked us to remove all pricing from the new content, and we have. There are no membership prices, no service prices, and no competitor prices anywhere in it. The only dollar figures that remain are your $10 million public liability insurance and the $50 off a first clean offer, both of which you confirmed were fine to keep.

One thing to flag: your **existing** live pages still display the membership price tiers and the End of Lease starting price. Those blocks sit in your website, not in the content we have written, so they will not disappear on their own when the new copy goes in.

**What we need from you:** please ask whoever manages the site to remove those existing pricing blocks at the same time the new content goes live. Otherwise the prices you asked us to take down will still be visible to visitors.

---

Happy to jump on a quick call if that is easier than replying point by point. Once we have your answers on points 1 to 3, any updates are a small change and we will turn them around quickly.

Thanks,
[name]
