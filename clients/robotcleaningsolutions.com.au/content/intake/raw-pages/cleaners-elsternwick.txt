We work with you to establish a secure access method that suits your comfort level. Many clients provide a spare key or lockbox code, which we handle with discretion and security.
Will the same cleaner attend every time I book a repeat service?
For members, we know that consistency is absolutely key to our service. You’ll have the same dedicated team for each visit, so that they understand your home’s specific needs and your personal preferences.
Are your cleaning supplies safe for households with young children and animals?
Absolutely. We use only premium-grade, eco-friendly products that are non-toxic and safe for your entire family, including pets.
Is it necessary for me to remain home, and will I need to provide supplies?
You don’t need to be home. Many clients prefer to return to a freshly cleaned house. We bring all professional-grade equipment and supplies, so you don’t need to provide anything.
How do you approach cleaning houses with indoor pets?
We’re highly capable with pet households and take special care to secure doors and gates. We’ll work around your pet’s routine and use pet-safe products throughout your home.
Am I able to give you a priority list for cleaning areas?
Certainly. We tailor our service to your needs and welcome priority lists to make sure we focus on what matters most to you during each visit.
