Frequently Asked Questions
How do cleaners get into my home if I'm away on the day of service?
We arrange secure key access methods that work for you. Many clients provide a lockbox code or leave keys with trusted neighbours. Your dedicated team will follow your preferred arrangement consistently.
For a scheduled recurring clean, is the same cleaner assigned every time?
Yes, absolutely. You’ll have your own dedicated cleaning team who visit every time. They learn your home’s layout, your preferences, and maintain consistent quality. It’s one of the key benefits of our membership program.
Do you also perform services such as oven cleans, rangehood cleaning, or glass cleaning?
Our deep house cleaning service includes detailed oven cleaning, window cleaning, and all glass surfaces.
Are your cleaning agents safe for children's toys and pet areas?
All our products are premium-grade, eco-friendly, and completely non-toxic. They’re specifically chosen to be safe around children, pets, and anyone with sensitivities.
Should I remain home, or do you bring all your own cleaning tools?
We bring all professional-grade equipment and supplies. You’re welcome to be home, but many clients prefer to be out while we work.
What's your policy when pets are in the house?
We’re comfortable working around pets. Just let us know about your furry friends during booking so our team can prepare accordingly.
Can the scope of cleaning be customised to suit my household?
Absolutely. Every home is different, from compact period cottages to sprawling modern residences. We tailor our service to your specific needs and preferences.
How long does it usually take to complete a 3-bedroom house cleaning?
With our teams working systematically, a standard 3-bedroom home typically takes 2-3 hours. Deep cleans or homes with heritage features may take longer to achieve proper care.
