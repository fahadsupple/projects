The Challenges of Deep House Cleaning in Aspendale
Living between the Nepean Highway and the foreshore means your home faces a constant assault from the elements. That fine salt spray carried by southerly winds doesn’t just make your windows hazy; it creates a corrosive film on all your surfaces. Meanwhile, no matter how careful you are, beach sand somehow finds its way into every corner of your home, grinding into your beautiful floors and carpets.
The higher coastal humidity also creates perfect conditions for mould and mildew, especially in bathrooms and laundries. Regular household cleaning products often struggle against these challenges, leaving you frustrated and spending entire weekends scrubbing without seeing real results.

Frequently Asked Questions
How do I arrange for the cleaner to access my house if I can't be there?
We work with many busy families who aren’t home during cleaning. We can arrange secure key collection or discuss lockbox options, whatever works best for you.
Will the same cleaner come each time if I set up a regular booking?
If you are a member with us, you’ll have a dedicated team that learns your home’s layout and your specific preferences. This consistency is one of the key benefits our members love most.
Do you provide add-on services such as carpet cleaning, oven cleaning, or window cleaning?
Yes, we offer add-on services perfect for tackling salt-affected windows or deep carpet cleaning to remove embedded sand. These can be added to your regular service or booked separately.
Are the cleaning products you use environmentally friendly and safe for kids and pets?
All our products are premium-grade, eco-friendly, and non-toxic. We’re committed to protecting both your family’s health and our beautiful coastal environment.
Am I required to be home during the cleaning, or can you bring your own supplies?
You’re welcome to be home if you prefer, but it’s not necessary. We bring all our own professional equipment and supplies, you just need to provide access to your home.
What's your approach to cleaning homes with cats or dogs?
We’re pet-friendly cleaners who understand that many homes include furry family members. We’ll work around your pets’ routines and make certain all products used are pet-safe.
Can I request specific cleaning tasks or create a list of focus areas?
Of course! While our checklist covers everything from dusting to floor mopping, we’re happy to prioritise specific areas that need extra attention, like salt-affected windows or sandy entryways.
How long does it generally take to clean a 3-bedroom house?
With our efficient teams, a typical 3-bedroom home takes 1.5-2 hours for a thorough clean. Deep cleans or homes with specific challenges like heavy salt build-up may take longer.
