Frequently Asked Questions
If I'm not home, how can your cleaners gain entry to the property?
We work with you to arrange secure access that suits your comfort level. Many clients provide a spare key or lockbox code. Our professional team is fully insured and trained in discretion, respecting your privacy and security at all times.
Will I have a consistent cleaner for my weekly or fortnightly service?
Our members can expect to have the same dedicated cleaning team for every visit. This builds trust, provides consistency, and means our team learns your specific preferences and home layout for increasingly efficient service.
Do you also perform one-off services like oven cleaning, carpet shampooing, or window washing?
Yes, we offer a variety of one-off services including deep oven cleaning and other specialised tasks. Contact us to discuss your specific needs and we’ll provide a tailored quote.
Are the cleaning products safe, biodegradable, and pet-friendly?
Yes, we exclusively use premium-grade, eco-friendly cleaning products that are safe, biodegradable, and non-toxic. Your family and pets stay completely safe, and we’re doing our part for the environment too.
Do I need to supply equipment like vacuums and detergents, or do you bring everything?
We bring everything needed for a thorough clean, including professional-grade equipment and our premium cleaning products. You don’t need to supply anything. Just leave it to us.
What procedures do you follow for cleaning houses with animals?
We have specific protocols for homes with pets, securing their safety while maintaining hygiene. This includes using pet-safe products and being mindful of pet areas and belongings during our service.
Can I adjust the scope of cleaning tasks based on my personal priorities?
Certainly. We understand every home and family has different needs. Let us know your priorities and we’ll customise our service accordingly.
On average, how much time is required for cleaning a 3-bedroom property?
Our efficient team typically completes a 3-bedroom home in 2-3 hours, depending on the specific requirements and size. We focus on thoroughness rather than rushing through a checklist.
