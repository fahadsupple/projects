Frequently Asked Questions
Will the same professional cleaner be assigned to my home for regular bookings?
If you’re a member of our 24/7 Clean program, you’ll have your own dedicated team who’ll learn your home’s specific needs and your personal preferences.
Do you rely on eco-friendly, family-safe products?
Yes, we exclusively use premium-grade, eco-friendly cleaning products that are tough on salt and sand but safe for your family and pets. No harsh chemicals, just effective, sustainable cleaning.
If I won't be home, how should I arrange access for the cleaning team?
We work with whatever arrangement suits you best. Many clients leave a key in a lockbox or provide entry codes. Your dedicated team is fully insured and trained in security protocols.
Can I create my own customised cleaning checklist?
Of course! While our standard service covers everything from cobweb removal to detailed bathroom cleaning, we’re happy to accommodate specific requests or priority areas.
On average, how many hours does it take to service a 3-bedroom house?
With our effective teams, a typical 3-bedroom home takes 2-3 hours for a thorough clean, including special attention to salt-affected areas and sandy entryways.
How do you verify pets are safe and comfortable during cleaning?
We’re pet-friendly cleaners who use only non-toxic products. We’ll work with you to make certain that your pets are comfortable, whether that means cleaning around their favourite spots or working in specific areas while they’re in another room.
