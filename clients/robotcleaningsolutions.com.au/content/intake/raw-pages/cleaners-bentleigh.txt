Frequently Asked Questions
What's the procedure if I want cleaners to enter my home while I'm away?
After our initial consultation, we can arrange secure key access, and our dedicated team will clean while you’re at work or otherwise occupied.
Do you assign the same cleaner for all regular visits?
Our membership model means you receive the same dedicated team for every visit. They’ll learn your preferences and maintain consistent quality–it’s like having your own personal cleaning team!
Beyond standard cleaning, can you also handle things like window washing or carpet steaming?
Yes, our deep house cleaning service includes window cleaning. We also offer specialised services like tile and grout cleaning, oven detailing, and seasonal deep cleans as part of our membership program.
Do your cleaning services pose any risk to children or pets?
Never. We exclusively use premium, eco-friendly products that are completely safe for your family and pets.
Is it mandatory for me to be present, and will I need to provide cleaning supplies?
You don’t need to be home, and we bring all our own premium supplies and equipment. Everything is included in our transparent pricing.
How do your staff manage cleaning in homes with pets?
Our teams are experienced with pets and work respectfully around them. We’re happy to follow any specific instructions regarding your furry family members.
Can I customise the cleaning checklist to highlight specific areas?
Of course. Your dedicated team will note your preferences and priorities, making sure that your home is cleaned exactly how you like it.
For an average 3-bedroom home, how long should I expect the service to take?
With our teams working systematically, a standard clean typically takes 1.5-2 hours for a three-bedroom home. Deep cleans may take longer depending on specific requirements.
