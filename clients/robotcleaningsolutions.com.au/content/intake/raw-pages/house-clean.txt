House Clean
At Robot Cleaning Solutions, we believe your home should be a sanctuary, not a source of stress. Between work, family commitments, and the fast-paced lifestyle of Melbourne’s Bayside and South-Eastern suburbs, finding the time for a thorough house clean is often a challenge. That’s where we come in. We bring a systematic, “robot-like” precision to your living space, ensuring every surface is spotless so you can spend your weekends enjoying the local beaches or cafes rather than scrubbing floors.
Our approach to residential cleaning is built on the philosophy of “White Glove Service.” This means we don’t just “wipe down” surfaces; we follow a rigorous, step-by-step protocol designed to catch the details that others often miss. From the top of the fridge to the grooves in your skirting boards, our team is trained to see what others overlook. Whether you need a regular weekly maintenance clean, a fortnightly refresh, or a intensive one-off deep clean, we adapt our services to fit your specific lifestyle.

What Our House Cleaning Includes:
All Living Areas:
Dusting all reachable surfaces, cleaning light switches, power points, and mirrors. We perform deep vacuuming and mopping with premium-grade equipment.
Kitchen Excellence:
Scrubbing stovetops, cleaning the exterior of all appliances, polishing stainless steel, and meticulously wiping down cupboard exteriors and splashbacks.
Bathroom Hygiene:
Disinfecting toilets, scrubbing tiles and grout to remove soap scum, cleaning mirrors, and polishing tapware to a high-end shine.
Eco-Friendly Safety:
Your family’s health is our priority. We use premium, non-toxic products that are safe for children and pets while still being incredibly effective.
By choosing Robot Cleaning Solutions, you aren’t just hiring a cleaner; you are hiring a dedicated team committed to the “Robot Standard.” Our cleaners are fully vetted, insured, and trained in-house to ensure consistency. With our membership options, you can enjoy the peace of mind that comes with having the same trusted team visit your home every time, allowing us to learn your preferences and deliver a truly personalised experience.
