Frequently Asked Questions
What arrangements are available for access when I'm not at home?
We work with many busy professionals who aren’t home during cleaning. We can arrange secure key access or work with your preferred entry method. Your privacy and security are paramount, and our professional teams are fully insured and background-checked.
Do I get continuity with the same cleaner for ongoing visits?
Members can enjoy having a dedicated cleaning team that learns their preferences and maintains consistent standards. This continuity is especially important for heritage homes where specific care requirements must be followed.
What's the approximate duration for cleaning a 3-bedroom dwelling?
Our productive teams typically complete a standard 3-bedroom home in 1.5-2 hours. Larger period homes or initial deep cleans may take longer. We work systematically to minimise disruption to your day.
Are your products environmentally safe and suitable for families with pets?
Yes, all our products are premium-grade, eco-friendly, and non-toxic. They’re specifically chosen to be safe for Australian families and pets while still being effective against coastal challenges like salt residue.
Can I specify tasks I want done each visit?
Of course. While we follow thorough cleaning protocols, we’re happy to prioritise specific areas or tasks that matter most to you. Your dedicated team will note your preferences for every visit.
