If I'm not home, what method do you use for entering my property?
We work with each client to establish a secure entry method that suits their comfort level. Many families provide a lockbox code or hidden key, while others prefer to be home during cleans. Your security and peace of mind are our priority.
Will the cleaner assigned to me stay the same for repeat visits?
Members of our 24/7 Clean program will have a dedicated cleaning team who learns their home and preferences.
Are your cleaning chemicals eco-friendly and safe for children/pets?
All our products are premium-grade, eco-friendly, and non-toxic. They’re specifically chosen to be safe for families while still being powerful enough to tackle coastal grime.
Do I need to supply anything, or do you come fully equipped?
We arrive fully equipped with professional-grade equipment and all necessary supplies. You don’t need to provide anything.
How do you approach households with active pets?
We’re seasoned with pets and work around them comfortably. Just let us know about your pets during booking so we can plan accordingly.
Can I choose specific rooms or areas for the cleaning focus?
Of course. While our standard service covers the entire home, we’re happy to focus on priority areas based on your needs.
What is the expected cleaning duration for a 3-bedroom house?
With our teams working systematically, a typical 3-bedroom home takes 2-3 hours for a thorough clean.
