Frequently Asked Questions
Do you send the same cleaner for all regular appointments?
Yes, we assign dedicated cleaning teams to our members. This confirms consistency, allows our team to learn your preferences, and builds the trust essential for a worry-free service.
Are your detergents environmentally responsible and safe around kids/pets?
We use only premium-grade, eco-friendly cleaning products that are non-toxic and safe for your entire family, including pets. These products effectively tackle Highett’s cleaning challenges without the need for harsh chemicals.
How long does it normally take to clean a standard three-bedroom home?
Our efficient teams typically complete a standard three-bedroom home in 1.5-2 hours. However, initial deep cleans or homes requiring extra attention may take longer. We’ll provide accurate timeframes during your consultation.
Will I need to remain at home, and do you bring cleaning supplies?
You don’t need to be home. Many clients provide access arrangements that suit them. We bring all professional-grade cleaning supplies and equipment, so you don’t need to worry about stocking anything.

Yes! We understand every home is unique. During your initial consultation, we’ll discuss your specific needs and create a tailored cleaning plan that addresses your priorities.
