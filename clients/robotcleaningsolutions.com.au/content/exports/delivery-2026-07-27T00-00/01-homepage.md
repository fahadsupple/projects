**What is the 20 minute rule of cleaning?**
The 20 minute rule is a popular habit of spending twenty focused minutes a day tidying to stop mess building up. It helps between visits, but it is no substitute for a systematic deep clean of bathrooms, kitchens and floors. Our members use the daily tidy for surface order and leave the thorough work to their dedicated team.

**Should I tidy before the cleaner arrives?**
There is no need to clean before we clean. A quick clear of personal items and clutter helps us reach more surfaces, but it is entirely optional. Your dedicated team will work around what is there and leave the home tidy and spotless.

**What do housekeepers not clean?**
Our standard clean focuses on the surfaces and areas you use every day, cleaning the exterior of appliances, cupboards and rangehoods rather than deep-cleaning inside them. Interior oven, fridge and dishwasher cleaning, grout scrubbing and internal cupboards belong to a deep clean rather than a standard visit. We do not carry out heavy decluttering, and anything outside a normal home clean is best discussed at your free quote so expectations are clear from the start.
