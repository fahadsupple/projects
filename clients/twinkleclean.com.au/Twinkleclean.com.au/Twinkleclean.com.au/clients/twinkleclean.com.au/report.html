Strategic Narratives

3 critical finding(s) for this run.

Fresh domain (DA 1) — suburb long-tail is the wedge

Twinkle Clean's domain authority is 1 with almost no ranking history. Head terms (commercial cleaning melbourne 1,900/mo) are out of reach short-term, but the 60 suburb pages target 10-320/mo long-tail where the incumbents (Whistle Clean DA16, GreenKleen DA17, MultiCleaning DA28) are thin — winnable within 3-6 months.

Carpet/steam silo is a pure content gap

The site has no carpet or steam-cleaning page at all — all 18 carpet suburb pages are net-new. Carpet suburb demand actually runs higher than commercial (carpet cleaning ringwood 110 vs commercial cleaning ringwood 10), so this 30% silo punches above its weight.

Local-pack + GBP is decisive for commercial suburbs

Commercial-cleaning suburb SERPs surface local packs; with DA 1, Twinkle Clean's fastest organic + map wins come from a complete Google Business Profile, consistent NAP citations (directory consent given), and suburb pages carrying the $20M PL, police-check and 20-year proof.
