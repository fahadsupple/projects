# Commercial Cleaning Cheltenham: Retail Tenancies and Industrial Sites

Two very different cleaning problems sit inside the one suburb here. On one side there is a retail tenancy where a customer's whole impression is formed at the entry glass and the fitting-room floor. On the other there is a warehouse office where the real issue is what the forklift traffic drags in off the concrete and onto the carpet by the door. Twinkle Clean handles commercial cleaning Cheltenham businesses need on both counts, with a scope written for the building you actually occupy rather than a generic checklist.

## Retail floors, and the Southland end of the suburb

Westfield Southland is the retail anchor for this part of Melbourne, and the trading pattern of everything around it follows the centre. Weekends are heavy, school holidays heavier again, and the standard a customer expects from a shop in Cheltenham is set by the centre next door whether you trade inside it or not.

Retail and shopfront cleaning here is largely a floors and glass job done properly:

- Hard floors scrubbed rather than smeared, the difference between a floor that looks clean under the ceiling lights and one that only looks clean at night
- Entry and display glass done with professional window-cleaning tools so no film is left in the corners
- Counters, handrails, fitting rooms, mirrors and the customer washroom worked through every visit

Where a tenancy has a food component, we degrease rather than just wipe, and the bin area is treated as part of the clean.

## The industrial belt on both edges

Cheltenham has a large industrial area along its eastern edge and a smaller one at the far western edge, and those sites are a genuinely different job. The floor is concrete or epoxy, not carpet.

The dust load is heavier and finer. The amenities block gets used by people coming off physical work, which puts a much bigger demand on washrooms and the crib room than an equivalent headcount in an office would.

So we separate the areas properly:

- Warehouse and workshop floors machine-swept or scrubbed on the frequency the traffic justifies
- Office and administration space in the same building cleaned as office space, with its own equipment, so warehouse grit is never carried onto the carpet
- Colour-coded microfibre cloths and mops keeping amenities, kitchen and general surfaces apart
- Staff lockers, lunch rooms and first aid rooms listed individually on the checklist so nothing gets assumed

The same office-and-industrial split turns up in Moorabbin too, which has its own [commercial cleaning in Moorabbin](https://www.twinkleclean.com.au/commercial-cleaning-moorabbin/) write-up.

The residential streets between the two industrial edges are mostly post-war housing built from the late 1940s into the 1980s, which is why many smaller trade and professional businesses here sit in converted or purpose-built premises rather than a single office park. Commercial cleaners Cheltenham operators call on need to be comfortable walking into all of it, and ours are.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do you clean washrooms and staff kitchens?**
Yes, and on most Cheltenham sites they decide whether staff think the clean is working. Washrooms get pans, urinals, basins, mirrors, dispensers and floors done with dedicated colour-coded equipment, then restocked. Kitchens and crib rooms get benches, sinks, tap ware, fridge and microwave fronts, tables and floors. Inside the fridge and microwave is a scheduled task on the frequency you choose.

**Do you disinfect high-touch surfaces?**
Yes. Door handles and push plates, light switches, EFTPOS terminals and counters, handrails, shared keyboards and phones, and washroom taps and flush buttons. We use hospital-grade disinfectants selected according to what the site requires, and give them the contact time the product actually needs rather than wiping them straight off.

**Do you handle waste and recycling removal?**
We empty and reline all internal bins and take the waste to your nominated external bin or bin store, keeping general waste and recycling streams separated as your building requires. Bins and the bin area are cleaned as part of the visit. The external collection contract stays with the site, and we work in with whatever schedule your provider runs.

**Can you clean a warehouse office and the warehouse floor as one job?**
Yes, and it is usually cheaper to have both done in one visit. We treat them as separate zones with separate equipment, so the office side is cleaned to office standard rather than being swept through with the warehouse gear.

**Do you cover retail tenancies near Westfield Southland?**
Yes, including the strip and standalone tenancies around the centre. Those sites usually want an early morning finish before trade, which we schedule as a matter of routine.

**Do you work on both the Bayside and Kingston sides of Cheltenham?**
Yes. The council boundary running through the suburb makes no difference to us. We service commercial sites across all of Cheltenham and the neighbouring bayside and Kingston suburbs.
