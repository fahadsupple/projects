## Frequently asked questions

**How often should carpets be professionally cleaned?**
Most homes are well served by a professional clean once a year, though busy households, pets or heavy foot traffic can make every six to twelve months a better rhythm. Rentals usually get done at the end of each lease, ready for the next inspection.

**Can you get out old or set-in stains?**
Often, yes. We treat stains as a separate step, working targeted spot and odour products into the mark before and during the steam clean, which lifts plenty of stains that have been sitting for a while. Some very old or dye-based marks can be permanent, and we will give you an honest read before we begin.

**Do you clean rugs and upholstery too?**
Yes. Alongside wall-to-wall carpet we clean rugs, including delicate and natural-fibre pieces, and we freshen lounges, chairs and mattresses through our upholstery cleaning, easily done in the same visit.

**Do you do bond-back carpet cleaning for units?**
Yes, and it is a large part of our Noble Park work. We clean to the standard a final inspection expects and back it with a bond-back guarantee, subject to the agreed scope and inspection conditions, giving tenants the best shot at their full deposit.

**Can you clean carpet in apartments?**
Yes. Many Noble Park homes are apartments and units, and we are set up for them: our equipment reaches upper-floor flats, and we plan around lift access and parking so the clean stays tidy and low-fuss for the neighbours.

**Do you clean carpet in older units and walk-up flats?**
Yes. A lot of Noble Park carpet sits in older units and walk-up blocks, and we clean it the same way we would a house, working around stairs and shared entrances so the job stays tidy for you and your neighbours.
