## Office cleaning across shared-building tenancies

Office cleaning Burwood tenancies need is mostly about consistency in the details that accumulate:

- Bins emptied and relined every visit rather than when they look full
- Kitchen benches, sinks and appliance fronts done properly, because a shared tea point is the first thing staff complain about
- Bathroom fixtures and touchpoints disinfected with hospital-grade product selected for that building
- Meeting-room tables cleared of marks, glass partitions cleaned so they read as glass rather than handprints, and vacuuming to room edges and under desk returns

In serviced and flexible suites the turnover of people is higher and amenities are shared, so touchpoint work carries more weight: door handles, light switches, printer panels and meeting-room controls. Colour-coded microfibre cloths and mops keep bathroom, kitchen and general-area equipment separate, which matters more on a shared floor than in a standalone building.

For sites with a duty of care to residents, students or families, we clean to the site's own written procedure rather than a generic list. Aged care and school clients usually have documented requirements already, and those become the checklist.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do you clean commercial carpet and hard floors?**
Yes. Commercial vacuums handle routine carpet work, and periodic carpet cleaning can be scheduled for corridors and high-traffic runs that dull faster than the rest of the floor. Hard floors are mopped with colour-coded equipment, with floor scrubbers and polishers used for periodic restoration on vinyl and tile.

**Do you clean internal windows and glass?**
Yes. Internal glass is part of standard office work: partitions, meeting-room walls, entry doors, reception screens and internal windows, cleaned with professional window-cleaning tools so they come up without streaking. External glass at height is quoted separately depending on access.

**Do you do end-of-lease cleaning for commercial premises?**
Yes. A commercial exit clean is a one-off job aimed at the condition your lease requires at handover: full floor treatment, internal glass, kitchens and bathrooms, marks on walls and skirtings, and residue left by fitout removal. Tell us what your make-good clause asks for and we will scope to it.

**Do you work inside Greenwood Business Park?**
Yes. Suites at 301 Burwood Highway, including serviced offices, are the kind of tenancy we clean regularly. Access and after-hours entry arrangements are agreed in writing before the first visit.

**Can you clean around Deakin term dates and school holidays?**
Yes. Institutions near the campus often want lighter work during teaching weeks and heavier work in breaks. We set that pattern up front rather than treating every visit identically.

**Is Burwood East part of your service area too?**
