## Retail and shopfront cleaning in a small-footprint village centre

Retail and shopfront cleaning around the station means tight tenancies with limited back-of-house, often with stock stored where storage was never really designed. The job comes down to a handful of things done properly and repeatedly:

- Window and door glass cleaned edge to edge with professional window-cleaning tools
- Floors vacuumed or scrubbed into the corners and under fixed shelving rather than along the obvious walking line
- Counters, handles, card terminals and any surface a customer touches disinfected rather than wiped
- Bathrooms and staff kitchens brought back to a standard your own team is comfortable with

Because these tenancies are small, we build the checklist tightly. A five-item generic list wastes a visit; a written list naming your actual fixtures means the crew covering a night your regular cleaner is away does the same job to the same standard. ## Frequently asked questions

### Are your cleaners insured and police-checked?
Yes to both. Every cleaner holds a current police check before they set foot on a client site, and the business carries $20 million public liability insurance along with WorkCover. We confirm this in writing as part of your quote.

### Are your staff trained in workplace safety?
Yes. Training covers workplace and chemical safety, including safe handling, dilution and storage of the products they use, along with our own site procedures and quality-control processes. Cleaners work to detailed checklists rather than deciding the job on the night.

### Do you use OH&S-compliant cleaning products?
We use commercial products handled according to their safety instructions, with staff trained in chemical safety, and hospital-grade disinfectants selected according to each site's requirements. Environmentally conscious products are used wherever appropriate for the surface. If your premises requires a particular product to be used or avoided, tell us at quoting and we will build it into the site procedure.

### Can you clean a small Blackburn shop with almost no back-of-house?
Yes, and it is a large part of what we do around the station shops. Crews bring their own equipment and consumables in and out, so nothing needs storing on site unless you prefer it kept there.

Yes. Box Hill is two stops from Blackburn and we work across both, so an owner with premises in each can run them on one schedule and one invoice.

### Our tenancy is in a heritage-feel building. Will that be an issue?
No. Older shopfronts often mean timber, painted trim and original glass needing a gentler approach than modern surfaces. We note surface types at the walkthrough and match products to them.

### Do you offer one-off cleans as well as regular schedules for small shops?
Yes. A lot of Blackburn's village shops start with a single deep clean before a busy trading period, then decide if a regular visit suits them. There is no lock-in contract either way.

### Will I get a written checklist of what's covered each visit?
Yes. Especially in a small tenancy where a five-item generic list wastes a visit, we write down exactly which fixtures and areas are covered, so a fill-in cleaner delivers the same standard as your regular one.
