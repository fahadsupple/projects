## Frequently asked questions

**Can you revive old, worn carpet in an established home?**
Often, yes. A lot of carpet in Moorabbin's older brick homes looks tired mainly because grit and oils have settled deep in the pile over the years. Hot water extraction flushes that build-up out and restores colour and texture the vacuum could never reach, though genuinely worn or damaged fibre has its limits, and we will tell you honestly what a clean can achieve before we start.

**Can you remove years of traffic marks from hallway carpet?**
Hallways and lounge entries take the heaviest wear in a family home, and those darkened traffic lanes are some of our most common jobs. We prespray and agitate the lane first to break up the compacted soil, then extract it with hot water, which lifts most long-standing traffic marks and evens out the pile.

**Do you clean villa units and townhouses?**
Yes. Alongside the older detached homes, Moorabbin has plenty of villa units and townhouses, and we clean carpet in all of them, from a single bedroom to a full multi-level property. Stairs and landings are handled with dedicated stair tools so the wear points get the same attention as the open floors.

**Do I need to move furniture before you arrive?**
It helps to clear small items, toys and anything breakable from the floors, and to lift what you easily can. Leave the heavier pieces to us, as our team will shift sofas and beds where it is safe to do so and clean around larger built-in furniture, so there is no need to empty the room.

**How soon can you come out?**
We clean seven days a week, including weekends and public holidays at no extra charge, so we can usually fit a Moorabbin job in within a few days, and sooner when a rental inspection or move-out date is looming. Give us a call with your timeframe and we will find a slot that works.

**What is the difference between steam and dry carpet cleaning?**
Steam cleaning, our hot water extraction method, injects heated water and solution into the pile then vacuums it straight back out, which reaches deeper soil and suits the older carpet in established homes. Dry, or low-moisture, cleaning uses far less water and dries faster, which can suit lightly soiled carpet or a quick refresh. We recommend whichever fits your carpet and its condition when we quote.
