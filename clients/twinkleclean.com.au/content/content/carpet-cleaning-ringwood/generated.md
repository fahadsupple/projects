**What is the difference between steam and dry carpet cleaning?**
Steam cleaning, or hot water extraction, sends hot water and solution deep into the pile and vacuums it straight back out, which is why it handles heavier soil and leaves the carpet only damp. Dry, or low-moisture, cleaning uses far less water and suits delicate fibres or a room that needs to be walked on again quickly. We choose between them based on the carpet in front of us.
