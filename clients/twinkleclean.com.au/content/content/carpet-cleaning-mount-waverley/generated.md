## Frequently asked questions

### How much does carpet cleaning cost?
It comes down to the number of rooms, the carpet's size and condition, and any stain or odour treatment involved. We quote for free, so the figure reflects your actual carpet rather than a flat rate.

### How much does carpet steam cleaning cost?
Steam cleaning, or hot water extraction, is our standard method, so it is already built into the quote on most jobs. Larger floors and heavier stain work add to the total, which is why we price on the details rather than a set list.

### Is professional carpet cleaning worth it?
For carpet carrying years of ground-in wear, it usually pays for itself. A professional clean restores colour and texture a domestic vacuum cannot recover, clears the odours that settle into an older pile, and adds life to the carpet you already have, which costs far less than replacing it.

### Can you revive decades-old carpet in a long-owned home?
Often, yes. Much of the carpet in Mount Waverley's long-held family homes looks tired mainly because grit and oils have worked deep into the pile over the decades, not because the fibre itself is finished. Hot water extraction flushes that build-up out and brings back colour and texture, though genuinely worn or damaged pile has its limits, and we will tell you honestly what a clean can achieve before we begin.

### Do you clean a whole house of carpet in one visit?
Yes. From a single bedroom to every carpeted room across a multi-level home, we handle the whole house in the one visit, and stairs and landings are done with dedicated stair tools so the wear points get the same care as the open floors.

### Can you lift worn traffic lanes in hallways?
The darkened lane down a hallway or in front of the couch is one of our most common jobs. We prespray and agitate the lane to break up the compacted soil, then extract it with hot water, which lifts most long-standing traffic marks and evens the pile back out. Deeply set wear may not vanish entirely, and we will be straight with you about the likely result beforehand.

### Can you gently clean a delicate natural-fibre rug in an older home?
Yes. The odd natural-fibre rug found in Mount Waverley's long-settled homes asks for a gentler touch than a hired machine can offer, so we read the fibre first and moderate the heat and water, which brings back colour and texture without stressing the backing.

### Do you treat musty odours in carpet that has been down for decades?
Yes. A faint mustiness that settles into the rooms with the most traffic is common in carpet this age, and we treat odours as their own targeted step alongside the main hot water extraction pass rather than relying on one general clean to shift it.
