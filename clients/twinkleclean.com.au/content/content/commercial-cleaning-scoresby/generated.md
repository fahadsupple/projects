## Shopfronts, floors and the customer path

Retail and shopfront cleaning is largely about the surfaces customers touch and look at. Entry doors and window glass get cleaned with professional window-cleaning tools rather than a spray bottle and paper towel, which is what leaves the streaks you notice at four in the afternoon when the sun is low. Floors are matched to the surface: vinyl and tile respond to machine scrubbing and periodic polishing, while carpet gets commercial vacuuming with attention to the traffic lane running from the door to the counter.

The counter itself, display units, shelf edges, EFTPOS terminals and door handles get wiped and disinfected, using hospital-grade disinfectants selected according to what each site requires and colour-coded microfibre cloths and mops so nothing carries from a washroom to a serving surface.

Back of house matters more than most people expect, because it is where staff spend their breaks and where an inspector looks first. Storerooms, staff kitchens, washrooms and bin areas all sit in the standard scope. Where your site runs to its own written cleaning procedure, our cleaners follow that document.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do you clean washrooms and staff kitchens?**
Yes, both are part of the standard scope. Washrooms get pans, seats, basins, taps, mirrors, dispensers and floors disinfected, with consumables restocked if you supply them. Kitchens get benches, sinks, splashbacks, appliance exteriors, cupboard fronts and floors, plus fridge interiors on a scheduled cycle if you want that included.

**Do you disinfect high-touch surfaces?**
Yes. On a retail site that means door handles and push plates, counter tops, EFTPOS keypads, display cabinet handles, light switches and staff room touch points. We use hospital-grade disinfectants chosen for the surfaces, and our cleaners are trained to leave them on the surface long enough to work rather than wiping them straight off.

**Do you handle waste and recycling removal?**
We empty bins throughout your premises, reline them, and consolidate waste into your external bins or the bin room your site uses, keeping general waste and recycling separated as your building requires. Removing waste off site entirely is a separate arrangement, and we say upfront what falls inside your quote and what needs your waste contractor.
