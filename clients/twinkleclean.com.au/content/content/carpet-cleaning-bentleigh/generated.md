### How long does carpet take to dry after cleaning?
Most carpets are dry within a few hours of a steam clean, since our extraction pulls the bulk of the moisture straight back out of the pile. Cooler weather, heavier fabrics and low airflow can stretch that out, and we can suggest walk-on and furniture timing for your carpet before we begin.
