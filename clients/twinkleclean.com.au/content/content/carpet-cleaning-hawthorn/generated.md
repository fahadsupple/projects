## Caring for carpets and rugs in period Hawthorn homes

Hawthorn grew up during the 1880s land boom, and its tree-lined streets still hold a fine collection of grand Victorian and Edwardian houses, including the heritage-listed Grace Park Estate. Homes of that era were often laid with wool and other natural-fibre carpets and rugs, and those fibres behave differently to modern synthetics: they hold warmth and depth beautifully, yet they mark more easily and dislike being over-wet.

That is why we assess the pile before we start rather than running one aggressive setting over everything. A period lounge with a delicate rug and a family entrance with hard-wearing carpet get treated as the two different jobs they are, and the leafy streets that make Hawthorn so pleasant also mean more pollen, grit and damp tracked indoors across the year.

## Method matched to the fibre, not a generic pass

Our core clean uses hot water extraction, the deep steam-cleaning method, which flushes soil out of the pile and draws the moisture straight back out so the carpet is left damp rather than soaked. On wool and softer natural fibres we can dial the approach back to a gentler, low-moisture treatment where the piece calls for it, which protects the fibre and shortens drying.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is steam cleaning safe for wool carpets and antique rugs?
Yes, when it is done with care. On wool and older pieces we check the fibre first and moderate the heat and moisture, often stepping down to a gentler low-moisture treatment, so a Hawthorn period home gets a thorough clean while the pile keeps its texture.

### Can you clean delicate or natural-fibre rugs?
Yes. Natural-fibre rugs mark easily and dislike being over-wet, so we match the treatment to the material and keep drying time short, which suits the wool and sisal pieces common in the suburb's established homes.

### Do you clean carpeted stairs in period homes?
Yes. Staircases in older Hawthorn houses take heavy foot traffic and hold soil in the edges and nosings, so we use dedicated stair tools to reach those tight spots and lift the worn look from each tread.

### How often should carpets be professionally cleaned?
Once a year suits most households, while busy family homes, pet owners and high-traffic hallways benefit from a clean every six to nine months. Regular cleaning also helps a good wool carpet last longer.

### Can you get out old or set-in stains?
Often, yes. Set-in marks get their own targeted treatment rather than a single pass, and while an aged stain cannot always be promised, we will tell you honestly what to expect after we assess it.

### Do you clean rugs and upholstery too?
Yes. Alongside your carpets we clean loose rugs, lounges, chairs and mattresses, so a period lounge room can be freshened in one visit rather than several.

### Do you clean carpet affected by pollen and leaf litter from Hawthorn's tree-lined streets?
Yes. The pollen, grit and damp that Hawthorn's leafy streets track indoors settle into carpet fibres over a season, and our hot water extraction lifts that ground-in debris along with everyday soil, rather than leaving it to dull the pile.

### Do you clean carpet in heritage-listed homes such as those in the Grace Park Estate?
Yes. Heritage-listed homes like those in the Grace Park Estate often carry original wool carpet or delicate runners, and we assess the fibre and finish before choosing a method, so the clean respects the age of the flooring rather than treating it as a modern synthetic carpet.
