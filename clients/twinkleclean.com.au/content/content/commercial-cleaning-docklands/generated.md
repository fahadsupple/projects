## Tower floors have their own rhythm

Docklands is a waterfront precinct at the western end of the CBD, built through urban renewal on what was once dock and light-industry land. What stands there now is contemporary architecture: mixed residential and commercial towers, with Southern Cross railway station and Docklands Stadium shaping how people move through the area. That geography matters for cleaning in practical ways.

Foot traffic arrives in concentrated bursts from the station, which loads the lift lobbies, entry mats and carpet runs near reception far harder than the rest of a floor. Full-height glazing facing the water shows every mark, and the light off the harbour is unforgiving about smears.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you work around our staff and trading hours?
Yes. Most of our Docklands work happens before 8am or after 6pm precisely so the floor is empty. If part of your team runs late or works weekends, we adjust the roster so cleaning happens where they are not.

Both. A one-off might be a post-fitout clean, a make-good before handing back a tenancy, or a reset before an investor visit. Regular schedules run daily, several times a week or weekly.

### Can we change the scope or frequency later?
Yes, and clients do. Tenancies expand, teams shift to hybrid weeks, meeting rooms get added. Tell us what changed and we reprice the schedule for the new arrangement.

We do. We work to the building's induction, dock booking and after-hours lift procedures, and we can hold access credentials under whatever arrangement management approves.

Yes. Shared kitchens that turn over an entire tenancy's lunch in ninety minutes are common in Docklands towers, and we can schedule a midday check alongside the main visit if the volume calls for it.
