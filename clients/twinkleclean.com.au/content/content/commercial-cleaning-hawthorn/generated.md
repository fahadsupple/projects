## Hawthorn offices sit above and behind the shopping strips

Hawthorn keeps its commercial life on named streets rather than in a business park. Tenancies sit above and behind the Glenferrie Road shops, along Auburn Road and Church Street, and through the Burwood Road corridor, which the council's Glenferrie Structure Plan and West Hawthorn Urban Design Framework treat as retail and commercial land. In practice that means small floorplates, shared stairwells, older glazing, and a loading situation that is often a two-minute window on a busy street.

Our office cleaning Hawthorn crews plan around exactly that. Equipment arrives on a trolley rather than a pallet, and where one corridor serves four suites, we agree in writing which parts of it fall inside your scope so nothing is left to assumption between neighbours.

## Consulting rooms, campus tenancies and schools each answer to a different standard

The suburb's largest single demand generator is Swinburne University of Technology's Hawthorn campus. Around it sit Glenferrie Private Hospital on Hilda Crescent, the Hawthorn Arts Centre, Boroondara's council offices in the former Hawthorn Town Hall, Scotch College and a dense cluster of other private and state schools. Add the restaurants, hotels and nightclubs Hawthorn is known for, plus some light industry on Burwood Road between Power Street and Auburn Road, and you get premises answering to very different expectations.

We deal with that by cleaning to your site's own written procedure rather than a generic checklist. Clinical and consulting spaces get hospital-grade disinfectants selected for the surfaces in that room, colour-coded microfibre cloths and mops so a bathroom cloth never reaches a desk, and a documented order of work. Teaching and admin areas get commercial vacuums, floor scrubbers and polishers across hard floors, and professional window-cleaning tools on the internal glass that shows fingerprints fastest.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does commercial cleaning cost?**
There is no single figure. The variables that move a Hawthorn quote most are floor area, how many bathrooms and kitchens are in scope, how often you want us on site, and how easy the building is to enter after hours. Send an approximate square metreage and a frequency and we can give you a realistic range before we visit.

**Can you clean after hours or on weekends?**
Yes. We operate seven days a week with no surcharge for weekend or public holiday work. Most Hawthorn offices we service run an evening slot from around 6pm, though early-morning starts suit some tenancies better.

**Do you require a lock-in contract?**
No. Work runs on an ongoing arrangement you can adjust or end when your circumstances change. Businesses stay because the standard holds, not because a document says they have to.

**Can you work around the Swinburne teaching calendar?**
Yes. Tenancies near the Hawthorn campus tend to swing hard between semester and break. We can lift frequency during teaching weeks and drop back over the quieter periods, with the schedule agreed in advance rather than renegotiated each month.

**Do you clean the internal glass on Glenferrie Road shopfronts?**
We do. Internal glass, entry doors, partitions and display windows are cleaned with professional window-cleaning tools. External high-level glass is assessed separately at the walkthrough so you know what is covered.

**Our Burwood Road building has no off-street parking. Is that a workable site?**
It is. Plenty of the tenancies we clean along Burwood Road and Auburn Road have kerbside access only. We bring compact equipment and confirm the access route with your building manager before the first clean rather than on the night.
