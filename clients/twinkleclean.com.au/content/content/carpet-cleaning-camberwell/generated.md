## Gentle steam cleaning for wool and natural fibres

The carpets that reward the most care in an established Camberwell home are usually the oldest: wool broadloom, natural-fibre runners and loose rugs handed down with the house. Those fibres mark more readily than synthetics and react badly to being over-wet. Our core clean is hot water extraction, the deep steam method, which flushes soil from the pile and draws the moisture straight back out, leaving carpet damp rather than soaked. On wool and softer fibres we ease back to a gentler, low-moisture treatment where the piece calls for it, and stubborn marks, pet odours and traffic lanes each get their own targeted step. ## Frequently asked questions

**Do you clean large period homes room by room?**
Yes, and it is how we prefer to work in Camberwell's bigger houses. A grand home has several carpet types under one roof, so we clean space by space, matching the treatment to the fibre and wear in each room rather than the whole floor to one setting.

**Can you care for wool and natural-fibre carpet?**
Yes. Wool broadloom, sisal and other natural fibres mark easily and react badly to heavy moisture, so we check the pile and backing first, then moderate the heat and water and often step down to a gentler low-moisture clean. That suits the suburb's older carpets and rugs.

**Do you cover nearby Boroondara suburbs?**
Yes. Alongside Camberwell we clean carpets across the surrounding area, including Canterbury, Balwyn, Surrey Hills, Glen Iris, Hawthorn and Kew.

**How much does carpet cleaning cost?**
There is no flat rate, because the cost tracks the number of rooms, the size and condition of the carpet, and any stain or odour work involved. A couple of rooms and a full grand house are very different jobs, so we quote on your carpet rather than a set price.

**How much does carpet steam cleaning cost?**
Steam cleaning, or hot water extraction, is our standard method, so for most jobs it is built into the quote rather than charged as an extra. Larger areas, delicate rugs and heavy stain treatment are what move the figure, which is why we price on the specifics.

**Is professional carpet cleaning worth it?**
For the wool carpet and rugs that fill an established Camberwell home, it usually pays for itself. A professional clean lifts the grit, allergens and traffic wear that dull good fibre, revives colour and texture across a whole house, and helps quality carpet last years longer instead of facing early replacement.
