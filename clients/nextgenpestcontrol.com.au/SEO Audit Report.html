Summary

Technical foundation is stronger than expected for a 40-page local-service WordPress site: zero broken pages, zero redirect chains, full HTTPS with a clean 301, every crawled URL is in the sitemap, every page is indexable, and desktop CWV are all "Good". The site runs WordPress + Yoast SEO + WP Rocket 3.21.3 (Delay JS already enabled) and SE Ranking's overall score is 81%.

However, two structural gaps significantly limit local-search performance. First, the JSON-LD on every page lacks the LocalBusiness/Service/AggregateRating schema this business needs — for a mobile-service pest controller with no street address, structured data is the only programmatic NAP signal Google has (SCHEMA-001, Critical). Second, every location and service page carries ~7 MB of unoptimised images on first load — desktop Lighthouse hides this, but SE Ranking's wall-clock crawler measured 4 pages at 1.9-4.0 s (TECH-002 / TECH-003 / CWV-001, High). The sitemap also shows no content has been refreshed since June 2025, an 11-month staleness gap that Phases 3 and 5 will pick up. Findings total: 1 Critical, 4 High, 4 Medium, 2 Low.

Critical Issues

SCHEMA-001: No LocalBusiness / Service / AggregateRating schema on any page

  • ID: SCHEMA-001
  • What: A local-service business operating across 20 Northern Beaches suburbs emits zero LocalBusiness, Organization, Service, or PestControlService JSON-LD. The only schema types present site-wide are generic WebPage, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, and FAQPage. Because the site uses a mobile-service model with no street address on /contact/ or the homepage, JSON-LD is the only programmatic way to communicate NAP + service-area to Google.
  • Evidence: mcp__playwright__browser_evaluate on the homepage returned 2 JSON-LD blocks (@graph: WebPage / WebSite / BreadcrumbList, plus FAQPage). Same on /pest-control-manly-vale/ (added an ImageObject node, otherwise identical) and the location page. Tested 2026-05-21.
  • Pages affected: 48/48 — site-wide (homepage, 7 hub pages, 40 suburb pages).
  • Impact: Per audit-issue-severity.md ("No LocalBusiness/Organization schema | Critical | Google cannot understand entity — impacts Knowledge Panel, local pack"). Direct blocker for Knowledge Panel construction, local-pack inclusion, and review-stars in SERP. Compounds with the absence of street address — Google currently has only the unstructured prose to triangulate this business.
  • Fix:
  • Add a site-wide Organization → LocalBusiness JSON-LD block (@type: "PestControlService") injected in <head> via Yoast (Schema settings → Organisation), or — for finer control — Schema Pro / Rank Math / wp_head filter. Required fields: name, telephone (1300 number), email, priceRange, areaServed (the 20 suburbs as a comma-separated string or an array of {@type: "City"}), serviceType, openingHoursSpecification, geo (centroid: lat -33.7, lng 151.30 for Northern Beaches), image, logo, sameAs (social profile URLs).
  • On every location page, append a Service block: provider references the LocalBusiness @id, areaServed is the specific suburb, serviceType: "Pest control", plus description.
  • Add aggregateRating to the LocalBusiness block ONLY once review counts are sourced from the Google Business Profile (Phase 4 will provide them) — never use self-served testimonials, per Google's review-snippet guidelines.
  • Validate with Google Rich Results Test + Schema.org validator before deploy.
  • Effort: Medium (4-8 hours; single template change, parametric per location page).
  • Priority: Critical.

  • What: Crawl-delay: 10 appears OUTSIDE any User-agent: group, BEFORE the User-agent: * declaration. Per the Robots Exclusion Protocol, every directive (including Crawl-delay) MUST be inside a User-agent group. SE Ranking's robots_has_errors flag fires on this.
  • Evidence: mcp__plugin_seoaudit_seranking__DATA_getAuditReport returned robots_has_errors.value: 1; mcp__playwright__browser_navigate to https://nextgenpestcontrol.com.au/robots.txt retrieved the full body (Chunk 3 includes the full file). Tested 2026-05-21.
  • Pages affected: site-wide (single file).
  • Impact: Spec-strict crawlers (Googlebot, Bingbot, lint tools) will ignore the orphan Crawl-delay. No paths are blocked, so this is not an indexation risk — but spec-strict third-party crawlers may bypass the requested politeness window, hammering the host. Recorded as Warning (not Critical) because no indexable content is blocked.
  • Fix: Edit robots.txt (via Yoast's tools panel or .htaccess override) to put Crawl-delay inside the User-agent group: ``` User-agent: * Crawl-delay: 10 Disallow:

Sitemap: https://nextgenpestcontrol.com.au/sitemap_index.xml ``` - Effort: Low (5 min). - Priority: High.

Title tag and meta description hygiene are the principal levers: 18 of 48 titles exceed Google's ~60-char SERP truncation point (37%) and 37 of 48 meta descriptions exceed ~155 chars (77%) — at site-wide impressions of 55,493 in 90 days, this is the single biggest lift opportunity in the audit. The 40-page location-pages template is structurally sound (unique H1s, hand-edited body openers, no duplicate titles, ~1,200-1,350 visible words per page) but lacks suburb-specific local facts (no landmarks, councils, climate, pest species) — the per-location uniqueness reads as keyword-token-swapping over a shared skeleton, which is a Phase 3 E-E-A-T concern that compounds the on-page CTR issue.

Findings total: 1 Critical, 5 High, 5 Medium, 4 Low. The Critical (META-001 + TITLE-001 together) is the only ceiling that can be unblocked in 1-2 weeks of editorial work; everything else flows from it. Cross-references to Phase 1 SCHEMA-001 (LocalBusiness schema) and Phase 5 cannibalization findings (already documented) are noted in Reconciliation.

Critical Issues

META-001: 37 of 48 meta descriptions exceed Google's SERP truncation point (77% of site)

  • ID: META-001
  • What: 37 of 48 pages (77%) have meta descriptions longer than ~155 characters — the point at which Google truncates the visible snippet in SERPs with an ellipsis ("…"). Of these, 8 exceed 170 characters (heavy truncation) and the worst is 190 characters (/pest-control-clontarf/). Truncated descriptions cut off mid-sentence and frequently drop the implicit CTA at the end. SE Ranking's description_long flag returned 30 of these (its threshold is more conservative); my analyst-side measurement against Google's ~155-char practical limit catches the additional 7.
  • Evidence: mcp__plugin_seoaudit_seranking__DATA_getAuditPagesByIssue(code="description_long") returned 30 URLs (full list in output/raw/02-chunk1-crawl-issues.md). My per-page measurement from the crawl data returned 37 URLs at >155 chars. Worst offenders: /pest-control-clontarf/ (190), /termite-control-narrabeen/ (182), /termite-control-seaforth/ (179), /termite-control-balgowlah/ (178), /termite-control-forestville/ (176), /pest-control-collaroy/ (172), /pest-control-cromer/ (171), /termite-control-mona-vale/ (171). 25 of the 40 location pages and 5 of the 8 hub pages are affected.
  • Pages affected: 37 of 48. Full list in checkpoint file.
  • Impact: This is the single biggest CTR lever in the audit. The site recorded 55,493 impressions in 90 days at 0.13% CTR (70 clicks). Position is mostly 20-50 (per Phase 5) where good snippet copy is the primary CTR driver — getting truncated drops 15-30% of the visible information and removes the implicit CTA. Conservative lift estimate: optimised meta at <155 chars with a hard CTA ("free quote", "1300 454 418") could move CTR from 0.13% to 0.5-1.0% (4-8x), yielding +200-400 incremental clicks/90d at zero ad-spend.
  • Fix:
  • Rewrite all 37 affected meta descriptions to 130-155 characters. Anchor each on: (a) the primary keyword ("Pest control in Manly Vale"), (b) one trust signal ("licensed experts" / "fully insured"), (c) a hard CTA ("Free quote — call 1300 454 418" or "Same-day service").
  • Template recommended for location pages: "{Suburb} pest control by licensed experts. Same-day service, eco-safe treatments. Free quote — call 1300 454 418." (~120-135 chars depending on suburb).
  • Template recommended for termite pages: "{Suburb} termite inspections & barrier treatments by licensed pros. Free quote — call 1300 454 418." (~100-115 chars).
  • Implementation-ready rewrites for the top 5 priority pages: see Data Tables → "Implementation-ready rewrites" below.
  • Update in Yoast SEO → per-page Meta Description field. Bulk-edit possible via Yoast bulk editor or the WP Bulk Edit for SEO plugin.
  • After deployment, request Search Console re-crawl on the top-10-impression pages (Search Console → URL Inspection → Request indexing) to accelerate re-cache. Monitor CTR in GSC for 14-28 days.
  • Effort: Medium (4-6 hours editorial; bulk-edit via Yoast).
  • Priority: Critical.
