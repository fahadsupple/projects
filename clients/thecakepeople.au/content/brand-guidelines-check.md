# The Cake People — Content vs Brand Communication Playbook

**Date:** 16 July 2026
**Content:** `Thecakepeople.au.docx` (revised, 24,171 words)
**Checked against:** `BRAND COMMUNICATION PLAYBOOK — The Cake People` (Insiteful, 55pp) — voice, personality, archetype, audience, positioning, USP, core messaging.

> This extends the earlier `brand-voice-check.md` (which only covered the 4 voice adjectives). The playbook adds **positioning, archetype and audience** — that's where the real gaps are.

## Verdict: strong on voice, care and authority — one genuine positioning conflict plus a few gaps.

The copy nails the two hardest things (Caring and Confident/authoritative) and handles the top objections. But it partly works *against* the playbook's central positioning rule — "keep the dietary/ethical angle in the background, lead with taste and design" — and it under-delivers the premium and cheeky/magical notes.

## Tensions & misalignment ⚠️

### 1. 🔴 Positioning conflict — 8 non-dietary pages LEAD with the dietary tag
The playbook is explicit: the brand **does not lead with the vegan/ethical angle** — "ethical considerations in the background", **"accidentally vegan"**, taste and design first, dietary as *reassurance*. Yet 8 H1s lead with the dietary label:

- Messi Cakes, **Fully Plant-Based** · Minecraft Cakes, **100% Plant-Based** · Peppa Pig Cakes, **Plant-Based** · Cupcakes, **100% Plant-Based**

**Nuance:** the *dietary collection* pages (gluten-free, egg-free, sugar-free) legitimately lead with the tag — that IS their search intent, so leave those. The problem is the **character/product pages**: a Minecraft-cake buyer wants the Minecraft hook first; "100% plant-based" belongs underneath as the reassurance. **The client's own live `/collections/vegan-cakes` page already does this correctly** ("we don't lead with plant-based… we win in mainstream categories") — so the new copy contradicts both the playbook *and* the existing site.
→ **Fix:** reorder those H1s so theme/design leads and plant-based follows as a sub-line.

### 2. ⚠️ Premium/upmarket positioning is under-hit and partly undercut
Audience = affluent Melbourne (South Yarra, Brighton), buying a **premium, "best of the best", status centrepiece**. But: "premium" appears only 4×; every "status" is *order-status*, not prestige; and the copy repeatedly anchors to a **budget** comparison — supermarket 10×, "packet cake", "quick birthday solution", "snack cake off the shelf." That frames the choice as "better than a $15 supermarket cake" rather than the upmarket showpiece the playbook wants.
→ **Fix:** swap the supermarket/packet contrasts for premium framing (the showstopper, the cake guests ask about).

### 3. ⚠️ "Cheeky" = 0
Explicit guideline: *"Our tone is often cheeky, aiming to bring a smile."* Still zero cheeky lines (same as the voice check). Also the Spice-Girls "bubbly, high-energy" note is only partly delivered.

### 4. ⚠️ Alchemist/Magician archetype (wonder, magic, transformative) is thin
magic 1×, transform 0×, enchant 0×. The "moments of wonder… magical memories" dimension of the core archetype is underdelivered (wonder 6× is the only real signal).

### 5. ⚠️ A top listed objection is never answered: "will I get what's in the photo?"
The playbook flags this as a key obstacle — *"no shopfront to see the cake in person; customers think images are retouched."* The copy addresses price, taste and delivery well, but **photo-accuracy / "what you see is what you get" = 0 mentions.** Easy, high-value reassurance line to add.

### 6. Minor — a couple of cause-led lines on product pages
"Proudly sustainable, planet-friendly" (KPop) and "sustainable plant-based ingredients" (Bluey) sit on *product* pages. The playbook wants causes in the background — move these to the About/story, keep product pages taste-and-theme first. (Volume is low; most "animal" mentions are "no animal products" = fine dietary reassurance.)

### 7. Hygiene — SEO scaffolding left inline
Some blocks still contain "Supporting keywords:" and "Meta Description:" lines in the body (gluten-friendly-cupcakes, sugar-free). Strip before publishing.

---

## Priority fixes
1. **Reorder the 8 dietary-led H1s** on character/product pages so theme leads, plant-based reassures underneath (leave the true dietary collections as-is). *Highest impact — it's the one place the copy fights the brand's own positioning.*
2. **Lift the premium register** — replace budget/supermarket contrasts with upmarket, best-of-the-best framing.
3. **Add a photo-accuracy reassurance line** to product/custom pages (answers a named top objection).
4. **Add cheek + a little wonder/magic** — at least one playful and one "wow" beat per page.
5. Move the 2 cause-led lines to the About/story; strip inline SEO scaffolding.
