# The Cake People — Replacement Keywords for Banned Terms

**Date:** 24 July 2026 (revised 27 July 2026 — new brand rule applied)
**Context:** Five keywords must be dropped, and the client has added a hard rule on how the replacements may be phrased. Volumes below are live Google Ads, Australia; intent confirmed on the live Melbourne SERP (DataForSEO).

## NEW RULE (27 Jul 2026) — read first

> **No keyword may _lead_ with a dietary descriptor.** The client will not target any keyword that starts with *dairy free, dairy friendly, sugar friendly, sugar free, plant based, egg free / eggless, gluten free / gluten friendly, or vegan.* The dietary strength stays in the body copy and brand voice — it is **not** the search target.

This **supersedes** the earlier "eggless" recommendation. Every "eggless" / "dairy-free-led" replacement from the first version is now withdrawn. All three replacements below lead with a **product or occasion** term, never a diet.

**⚠️ Knock-on effect the client should be aware of:** four keywords **already selected** in the current plan (Silo 3) also break this rule and should be reviewed under the same logic — `gluten free cakes melbourne`, `dairy free cakes melbourne`, `sugar free cakes melbourne`, `gluten free birthday cake melbourne`. They lead with a dietary descriptor exactly like the vegan terms. Flagging so the rule is applied consistently, not just to the 3 vegan terms. (See "Consistency check" at the bottom.)

## The 3 replacements (product/occasion-led, no dietary word in the lead)

### 1. vegan cupcakes melbourne (90) → **cupcakes melbourne** (1,300) ✅ the standout win
- **Primary:** `cupcakes melbourne` — **1,300/mo**, HIGH commercial competition.
- **SERP validated (live, Melbourne):** 100% commercial — a 3-pack local pack (The Cupcake Queens, Little Cupcakes, Cupcake Central) plus every organic result is a cupcake shop or ecommerce store (Little Cupcakes, Cupcake Central, Ferguson Plarre, Goldelucks ranking a `/collections/cupcakes` page, The Cupcake Queens). **Zero recipe contamination.** The Cupcake Queens (DA 20) is already a mapped competitor — exactly the right competitive set.
- **Secondary (same page):** `cupcake delivery melbourne` (480) — matches the Melbourne-wide delivery / Click & Collect model.
- **Page:** `/collections/plant-based-cupcakes`.
- **Note:** this is a big upgrade, not a downgrade — the dietary term it replaces was only 90/mo; the clean product head is **1,300**. The page keeps "plant-based" as its on-page voice; the meta title / H1 lead simply becomes *Cupcakes Melbourne* instead of a diet word.

### 2. vegan cakes melbourne (720) → **celebration cakes melbourne** (170) + cakes melbourne (5,400)
- **Primary:** `celebration cakes melbourne` — 170/mo, geo-commercial, and the natural non-dietary label for this collection.
- **Head/stretch secondary:** `cakes melbourne` (**5,400**) and/or `cake shop melbourne` (3,600) — the category heads this collection can also target. These are big but generic and high-competition (site DA 17); realistically a long-term play, and the **homepage** may be the better owner of the single head term. Dev/client to decide whether the head lives on the homepage or this collection.
- **Consolidation note:** `celebration cakes melbourne` is **already a Silo 4 keyword** in the current plan. Rather than run a separate Silo 4 page and this collection against the same term, point them at **one** page (this collection is the logical home). Otherwise two of the client's own pages compete for it.
- **Page:** `/collections/plant-based-cakes`.

### 3. vegan birthday cakes melbourne (110) → **birthday cake delivery melbourne** (480) *(decision needed)*
- **The honest problem:** once no dietary word can lead, **every clean non-dietary birthday term is already taken** — `birthday cakes melbourne` (2,900) is Silo 4, `kids birthday cakes melbourne` is Silo 2, and the milestones (16th/21st/40th/60th/70th) are Silo 2. There is no unused, non-dietary, birthday-specific keyword left to give this slot without duplicating an existing page.
- **Recommended replacement:** `birthday cake delivery melbourne` (**480/mo**) — non-dietary, occasion-led, real volume, and fits the delivery model. Best placed on the birthday collection or the planned `/pages/cake-delivery-melbourne` hub. (Intent overlaps `birthday cakes melbourne` + `cake delivery melbourne`, so keep it as a supporting term on one of those pages, not a third competing page.)
- **Cleaner-but-smaller alternative:** `number cakes melbourne` (50) — a distinct milestone-number product format, non-dietary, and **not** currently in the plan; zero overlap, but low volume.
- **Or:** retire this slot entirely and let the existing `birthday cakes melbourne` page carry the birthday-dietary audience through body content — freeing the 30th slot for any new term the client prefers.
- **Page:** birthday collection / delivery hub.

## Net effect

The no-dietary-lead rule actually **increases** the addressable volume on two of the three pages, because clean product/occasion heads out-pull the dietary long-tail:
- Cupcakes: **90 → 1,300** (cupcakes melbourne), + 480 delivery secondary.
- Cakes: 720 (vegan) → 170 clean primary, with the **5,400** category head as upside.
- Birthday: 110 (vegan) → 480 (delivery) or a clean 50 (number cakes).

**The trade-off to name plainly:** these three pages no longer capture *dietary-specific* searchers through the keyword itself. That demand (people typing "vegan/dairy-free/gluten-free cakes melbourne") now has no owning keyword in the plan — it's served only via body content and the character/milestone pages. That is the direct consequence of the rule, and it's the client's call to accept it.
