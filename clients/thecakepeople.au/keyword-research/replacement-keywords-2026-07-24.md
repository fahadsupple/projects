# The Cake People — Replacement Keywords for Banned Terms

**Date:** 24 July 2026
**Context:** Five keywords must be dropped (brand rules: no "vegan," no "custom," and the Messi pages removed). Volumes below are live Google Ads, Australia; intent confirmed on the live Melbourne SERP (DataForSEO).

## What's being replaced

| Dropped keyword | Vol/mo | Why dropped |
|---|---|---|
| vegan cakes melbourne | 720 | "vegan" banned |
| vegan cupcakes melbourne | 90 | "vegan" banned |
| vegan birthday cakes melbourne | 110 | "vegan" banned |
| messi cakes | 170 | living person's name/likeness |
| custom cakes melbourne | 1,000 | "custom" banned + strategy moving off custom |

**Already decided by client:** `soccer cakes melbourne` + `football cakes melbourne` become the football-collection keywords (covering the Messi slot and absorbing the custom-cake budget/intent). Volumes + upside for those confirmed at the bottom.

**This document supplies the remaining 3** — replacements for the three "vegan" keywords.

## The key finding (read this first)

Once "vegan" is off the table, **"eggless" is the strongest brand-safe replacement term, and "plant based" / "egg free" are nearly dead in search:**

| Term | Vol/mo | Competition |
|---|---|---|
| **eggless cake / eggless cakes** | **2,900** | MEDIUM (winnable) |
| **eggless cupcakes** | **720** | MEDIUM |
| eggless cakes melbourne | 140 | high |
| dairy free cakes melbourne | 110 | high |
| eggless birthday cake | 90 | high |
| plant based cake / cakes | 90 | high |
| **plant based cakes melbourne** | **~0** | — |
| **plant based cupcakes melbourne** | **~0** | — |
| egg free cakes melbourne | 10 | high |

The pages were renamed to **plant-based** — good for brand voice, but **"plant based cakes melbourne" has ~0 search volume** while **"eggless" pulls 140 (geo) up to 2,900 (national)**. Recommendation: keep "plant-based" as the on-page brand language, but **lead the meta title / H1 / target keyword on "eggless" + "dairy-free"**, which is what people actually search. "Eggless" is fully on-brand (their kitchen is 100% egg-free) and its Melbourne SERP is exactly the eggless/vegan specialist bakeries — the right competitive set for The Cake People.

**SERP intent rule confirmed:** geo-qualified "…melbourne" terms return clean commercial SERPs (bakeries, local pack, "near me"); the generic national terms (eggless cake, eggless cupcakes, dairy free birthday cake) are partly recipe-dominated — so target the geo term as primary and the big generic term as a secondary the page can also rank for.

## The 3 replacements

### 1. vegan cakes melbourne (720) → **eggless cakes melbourne** (140)
- **Primary:** `eggless cakes melbourne` — 140/mo, **SERP is 100% commercial** (Isher Eggless Bakers, Mahadeva's, Nanak, Mister Nice Guy's, Brunetti, local pack, PAA "where can I find eggless cakes…"). Google shows near-identical results for "eggless cakes melbourne" and "vegan cakes melbourne," so this captures the same buyers without the banned word.
- **Secondaries (same page):** `eggless cake` / `eggless cakes` (**2,900**, MEDIUM competition — big upside; part recipe but a strong Shopping layer), `dairy free cakes melbourne` (110, already in the plan).
- **Page:** `/collections/plant-based-cakes` — set meta title/H1 to lead on *Eggless & Dairy-Free Cakes Melbourne*.

### 2. vegan cupcakes melbourne (90) → **eggless cupcakes melbourne** (+ dairy free cupcakes melbourne)
- **Primary:** `eggless cupcakes melbourne` — brand-safe, clean local intent, captures the "near me" cupcake buyers.
- **Secondary:** `dairy free cupcakes melbourne` (10).
- **Honest caveat:** the cupcake-dietary category has very little *clean* commercial Melbourne volume once "vegan" is removed. The high-volume `eggless cupcakes` (720) is **recipe-dominated** (validated — organic is all recipe blogs + a Recipes carousel + "how to make cupcakes without eggs" PAA), so it's not a clean primary; treat it as a stretch secondary only. Realistically this is a small slot — the plant-based cupcakes page is best seen as a dietary filter page rather than a volume driver.
- **Page:** `/collections/plant-based-cupcakes`.

### 3. vegan birthday cakes melbourne (110) → **dairy free birthday cake melbourne** (+ eggless birthday cake)
- **Primary:** `dairy free birthday cake melbourne` — clean local intent; "dairy-free" is a client-approved, accurate term.
- **Secondaries:** `dairy free birthday cake` (**260** — highest-volume of the birthday-dietary options; SERP is mixed recipe/commercial but with a strong Melbourne Shopping + local-pack layer where The Cake People's products can surface), `eggless birthday cake` (90).
- **Page:** the plant-based cakes collection, or a birthday-dietary section of `/collections/birthday-cakes`.

## Net effect

Removing the 5 banned terms strips ~2,090/mo of *targeted* volume (of which custom cakes melbourne = 1,000 is a deliberate strategic exit). The brand-safe replacements recover it and then some through the national secondaries — **eggless cake (2,900), soccer cake (1,900), eggless cupcakes (720), football cake (590), soccer birthday cake (390), dairy free birthday cake (260)** — all of which the relevant collection pages can rank for, with clean geo-primaries anchoring local intent.

**Two things to confirm with the client:**
1. Comfort with **"eggless"** as the lead search term on the dietary pages (it's accurate and brand-safe, but it's a shift from the "plant-based" language they chose for voice).
2. That dropping **custom cakes melbourne (1,000/mo)** entirely is accepted — it's consistent with the strategy of moving off custom, but it's the single biggest volume being given up.
