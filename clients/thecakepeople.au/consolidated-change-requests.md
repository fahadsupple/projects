## 2. Strategic direction (Request 1 — drives campaign/funnel build, not just wording)

- The business is **"deliberately moving away from one-off custom cakes."** Sales & marketing focus is the **three high-margin ranges: edible image cakes, vintage cakes, and the signature celebration range.** Advertising should **"groom customers toward these ranges from the very first touch."**
- **"We don't customise anything unless it can be customised on the website, within the options and limits we offer online. The funnel should lead people to product pages where they make those choices themselves."**
- **"We want to minimise phone conversations, not generate them. Ads should drive to product pages, not to enquiry calls."**
- **No custom cake advertising (Request 2, item 6):** Remove the custom cakes page and the custom/bespoke framing across the other pages. *"We do make custom cakes, but we're not advertising them, and we're not directing people into phone conversations to discuss requirements. Everything should point customers to product pages where they personalise within the options we offer online."*
