DATA-QUALITY ISSUES FOUND WHILE BROWSING
================================================================================================

a) FROZEN CATEGORY IS BROKEN — every Frozen product 301-redirects to the
   category page, which shows no products. In a random sample of 160 products
   from the sitemap, 12 (7.5%) redirected this way; the Frozen brand filter
   reports 246 products. So roughly 246 products are unreachable and
   unsellable. This is the single biggest issue on the site.
   Affected examples: martins-*-potato-rolls, ravifruit-frozen-*-puree,
   careme-butter-puff-pastry-sheets, mission-wheat-tortilla,
   georges-bruck-semi-cooked-foie-gras, borde-frozen-mushroom-mix,
   buvetti-italian-frozen-pizza-doughballs, big-country-ultra-crisps.

b) <title> TAG WRONG ON FROZEN PRODUCTS — they return "Frozen | Foodistribute"
   instead of the product name, because the URL resolves to the category.

c) SLUG TYPO: /product/varlhona-dulcey-32-chocolate-bar-70g/ — "varlhona".
   The product name is correct ("Valrhona Dulcey 32% Chocolate Bar"). This is
   exactly why slug-matching alone would have missed it.

d) SLUG/NAME MISMATCH: /product/agina-ibarra-peppers-guindillas-4kg/ has the
   on-page name "Agina Eusko Peppers (Guindillas) - 1.85kg". Slug says Ibarra
   and 4kg, page says Eusko and 1.85kg. Worth the client checking which is
   right.

e) THREE VALRHONA PRODUCTS WOULD HAVE BEEN MISSED by name/slug matching alone,
   and were only found by browsing the catalogue:
     /product/praline-50-crunchy-hazelnut-almond-praline-5kg/
       -> "Valrhona 50% Crunchy Hazelnut & Almond Praline - 5kg"
