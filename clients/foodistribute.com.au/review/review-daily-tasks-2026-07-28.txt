ADDENDUM — SITE-WIDE COLLECTION PAGE SWEEP (28 July 2026)
================================================================================
Scope: every product-category (collection) page on the site — all 61 URLs in
product_cat-sitemap.xml, cross-checked against every category linked from the
main nav and /shop-by-category/. No orphaned categories found; the two lists
matched exactly, so 61 is the complete set.

Method: fetched each page, counted rendered product tiles, checked for the
"No products were found matching your selection" empty state, and cross-checked
against the "Shop by Brand" filter counts (a populated category always exposes
brand facets with counts; a genuinely empty one exposes none).

RESULT: 13 of 61 collection pages have no products showing, plus 1 that is
broken. Beyond the 10 already reported, THREE more issues:

11. https://foodistribute.com.au/product-category/uncategorized/
    0 products, shows "No products were found matching your selection".
    This is the WooCommerce default catch-all term. It is set to index,follow
    and is included in the XML sitemap.
    ACTION: delete the term, or at minimum noindex it and pull it from the
    sitemap. It should never have been crawlable.

12. https://foodistribute.com.au/product-category/xmas-range/
    0 products, shows "No products were found matching your selection".
    Seasonal category, presumably emptied after Christmas. Set to index,follow
    and in the XML sitemap.
    ACTION: if it will be repopulated each December, noindex it while empty
    rather than leaving a dead commercial page live year-round. Note the site
    also has a separate /product-category/christmas-deals/ which IS populated
    (18 products) — worth confirming with the client whether both are needed
    or one should be retired/redirected.

13. https://foodistribute.com.au/product-category/pantry-grocery/frozen/
    >> DIFFERENT PROBLEM — this one is BROKEN, not empty.
    The category HAS products: the Shop by Brand filter lists 29 brands
    totalling 246 products (Gelista 13, Sunnyside 12, Bone Roaster 9,
    Mission 9, Lamb Weston 4, Amazonia 4, etc.).
    But the page renders NO product grid at all — zero product tiles, no
    pagination, and not even the "No products were found" message that a
    genuinely empty category shows. Confirmed identical across repeat fetches,
    so it is not a caching blip. By comparison the sibling category
    /pantry-grocery/chilled/ renders its grid normally.
    A customer landing on Frozen sees the filters and the sidebar and nothing
    to buy.
    ACTION: developer to investigate why the product loop fails to output on
    this term. Highest-priority of the three — this is 246 sellable products
    that cannot be reached from their own category page.
