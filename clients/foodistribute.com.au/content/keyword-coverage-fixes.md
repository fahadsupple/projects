## 1. `/cafe-and-restaurant-food-suppliers/` — FAQ answer
**Fixes existing keywords:** cafe food suppliers melbourne, restaurant food suppliers melbourne
**Why:** Melbourne is never adjacent to the keyword — only "cafe food suppliers in Sydney and Melbourne". Sydney adjacency must be preserved, so keep sentence one untouched and append sentence two.

**Find:**
> Yes. We are well-established cafe food suppliers in Sydney and Melbourne, and restaurant food suppliers in Sydney and Melbourne, providing the specialty pantry stock required for high-volume service.

**Replace with:**
> Yes. We are well-established cafe food suppliers in Sydney and Melbourne, and restaurant food suppliers in Sydney and Melbourne, providing the specialty pantry stock required for high-volume service. As cafe food suppliers in Melbourne and restaurant food suppliers in Melbourne, we supply the same specialty range across Melbourne.
