# Foodistribute — Keyword Coverage Check (new content vs Meta File)

**Date:** 13 July 2026
**Content doc:** `Foodistribute.com.au (Approved).docx` (30 pages)
**Meta file:** `Fooddistribute.com.au-Keyword-URL-Meta.xlsx` → sheet "Meta Data (For Hardys Content)"
**Method:** parsed bold formatting in the Keywords column to separate NEW (bold) from EXISTING (non-bold), then phrase-matched every keyword against that page's Meta Title, Meta Description, H1, subheadings and body copy.

## Verdict

All 30 URLs in the meta file have content written. Every existing (non-bold) keyword's **primary term is still held in the Title and H1** on its page — no page has dropped its old primary in favour of the new one. 5 items need a fix before this content replaces what's live.

Most "misses" my check flagged are just a filler word inserted, e.g. the page says *"specialty food supplier **in** Australia"* rather than *"specialty food supplier Australia"*. Those read naturally and Google treats them as the same phrase — no action needed.
