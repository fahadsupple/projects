## Louvred Pergolas Melbourne

The heart of the range is the louvred roof: a bank of aluminium slats you tilt to let in sun or shade, or close over completely against a downpour. On the motorised models a remote runs the slats, with perimeter lighting fitted as standard and no upcharge. The frame is high-grade aluminium on stainless steel hardware with a dipped powder-coat finish, so it lives outdoors through summer heat and winter wet with effectively no maintenance and no rust.

The Delta line covers the choices most Melbourne yards call for. The [Delta Light Motorised](https://skyflex.au/product/delta-light-motorised/) is the entry point. The [Delta Motorised](https://skyflex.au/product/delta-motorized/) and [Delta Open Sky](https://skyflex.au/product/delta-open-sky/) sit in the mid-range, and the Open Sky is the one whose blades both rotate and fully retract, opening the roof right back to the sky. Alongside the louvred pergolas the range runs to the [Delta Pro retractable roof](https://skyflex.au/product/delta-pro-retractable-roof/), [folding-arm awnings](https://skyflex.au/product/delta-commercial-folding-arm/), an [outdoor 4K TV](https://skyflex.au/product/skyflex-4k-android-smart-outdoor-tv/), [BBQ pods](https://skyflex.au/product/skyflex-bbq-pods/) and more, so one project can cover the whole outdoor room.
