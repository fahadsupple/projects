# Pergolas Melbourne: Louvred Outdoor Living Made for the Local Climate

Melbourne can hand you four seasons before lunch, and an outdoor space that only works on a still, sunny day sits empty most of the year. Skyflex builds adjustable louvred pergolas Melbourne homeowners and businesses use right through that variability: rotate the slats open for a warm afternoon, close them flat when the rain sweeps in, and the room underneath stays usable. We are a Melbourne outdoor-living brand with a showroom in Epping, where you can stand under the full Delta range before you decide. Every structure is aluminium, motorised, and made to the size, setting and colour of your space.

## A louvred pergola range built for Melbourne weather

The heart of the range is the louvred roof: a bank of aluminium slats you tilt to let in sun or shade, or close over completely against a downpour. On the motorised models a remote runs the slats, with perimeter lighting fitted as standard and no upcharge. The frame is high-grade aluminium on stainless steel hardware with a dipped powder-coat finish, so it lives outdoors through summer heat and winter wet with effectively no maintenance and no rust.

The Delta line covers the choices most Melbourne yards call for. The Delta Light Motorised is the entry point. The Delta Motorised and Delta Open Sky sit in the mid-range, and the Open Sky is the one whose blades both rotate and fully retract, opening the roof right back to the sky. Alongside the pergolas the range runs to the Delta Pro retractable roof, folding-arm awnings, an outdoor 4K TV, BBQ pods and more, so one project can cover the whole outdoor room.
