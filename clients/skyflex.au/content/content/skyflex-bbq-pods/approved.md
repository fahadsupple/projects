## How a made-to-order BBQ pod comes together

Most pods are sold as a finished unit in a set size. Skyflex works the other way around. The pod starts as a consultation: you tell us the footprint you have, how you like to cook and host, and the look you are after, and we help you settle the configuration from there. Once the size, layout and finish are agreed, the pod is built to that specification and delivered to your address. Because it is a custom order rather than an off-the-shelf box, nothing is locked in until you have signed it off. That is also why it is not sold for direct online purchase: the price and the build both follow the choices you make with the team.
