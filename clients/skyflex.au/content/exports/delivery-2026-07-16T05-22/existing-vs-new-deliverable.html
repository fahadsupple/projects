Full sun or partial shade? Start here

Outdoor TVs are not one category. The market splits by how much direct sun a screen can fight, and brightness sorts them. A typical indoor television runs near 300 nits. This Skyflex panel puts out 1000 nits, roughly three times that, enough to stay clear in a covered outdoor room, a roofed alfresco, a verandah, or a corner that only catches glancing light.

What it is not built for is a wall in open, unshaded sun through the middle of the day. Screens made to win that fight run at 2000 nits and beyond, and they cost accordingly. Being honest about it saves you a return. If your spot sits under a pergola, an awning, a louvred roof or a patio cover, 1000 nits does the job. If it faces raw sun with nothing overhead, choose a brighter class of panel. A backyard that already has a shaded entertaining area is exactly where this TV belongs.

What "weatherproof" means here, and what IP55 really covers

A weatherproof TV earns the name through sealing, not marketing. This one carries an IP55 rating. The first 5 means dust cannot get inside in a harmful amount. The second 5 means it withstands low-pressure water jets from any direction, so sideways rain, a hose-down, or a splash from the pool nearby stays out of the electronics. It lives outside through the seasons rather than coming in when the sky turns.

IP55 is a solid, mid-pack outdoor seal. Some screens push to IP65 for a tighter dust seal, worth knowing if you are comparing spec sheets, though for the covered spaces 1000 nits already points you toward, IP55 is matched to the job. The built-in speakers are sealed to the same standard, so sound comes off the unit without a separate enclosure.

What a smart toilet actually does

A smart toilet, sometimes called an intelligent toilet, an electric bidet or a Japanese toilet, replaces paper-and-flush with a set of electronic functions built into the pan or the seat. The features people talk about most are the practical ones. A heated seat, so the first contact on a cold Melbourne morning is not a shock. On top of those, most models add an auto open-and-close lid, a soft night light, and a self-cleaning or deodorising cycle.

Two body styles cover almost everything on the market. An integrated smart toilet suite is a single unit where the pan, cistern and electronics are designed together. A bidet seat is an electronic seat that bolts onto a compatible pan you may already own, which is the cheaper way in and a common first step.
