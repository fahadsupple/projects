# Open questions for the client

Raised during the content pipeline and still unresolved. Each one is a place where the
pages were written conservatively because the answer was not available, so a client answer
either unlocks stronger copy or confirms the cautious version is correct.

Last updated: 2026-07-31 (during `/content:generate`, at 11 of 41 pages)

---

## 1. Is `globallocksmiths.net.au` yours?

**Status:** unresolved, and it matters most of the three.

A second domain using the client's brand name appears across search results for at least
**12 target suburbs**, with page titles reading "Global Locksmiths" and a full regional
path structure:

```
globallocksmiths.net.au/eastern-suburbs/locksmith-hawthorn/
globallocksmiths.net.au/northern-suburbs/locksmith-ascot-vale/
globallocksmiths.net.au/northern-suburbs/locksmith-flemington/
globallocksmiths.net.au/northern-suburbs/locksmith-moonee-ponds/
globallocksmiths.net.au/southern-suburbs/locksmith-prahran/
globallocksmiths.net.au/western-suburbs/locksmith-deer-park/
globallocksmiths.net.au/western-suburbs/locksmith-docklands/
globallocksmiths.net.au/western-suburbs/locksmith-footscray/
globallocksmiths.net.au/western-suburbs/locksmith-hoppers-crossing/
globallocksmiths.net.au/western-suburbs/locksmith-point-cook/
globallocksmiths.net.au/western-suburbs/locksmith-werribee/
globallocksmiths.net.au/western-suburbs/locksmiths-altona/
```

Evidence collected: it is indexed by Brave, its snippets advertise round-the-clock
emergency service (a claim the client has confirmed they do NOT offer), and the domain
**does not resolve** via DNS from here. The analyst has confirmed the client's site is
`globallocksmiths.com.au`, so this is not theirs in the current sense.

Most likely a lapsed former domain (the business previously traded as Newmans Locksmiths)
or brand squatting. Either way it is ranking on the client's brand name across a dozen
suburbs we are writing pages for.

**Ask:** did the business ever own this domain, and does anyone still control it?

## 2. Will you fit a smart lock the customer bought themselves?

**Status:** assumed yes, on the plan's authority rather than a client statement.

`client-profile.json` lists "Smart Lock Installation" and "Lock Fitting and Servicing" but
carries **no explicit customer-supplied-hardware policy**. The locked plan mandates the
topic on every page, and the suburb research shows it is one of the most common live
questions across the grid (Braybrook, Maribyrnong, Hoppers Crossing, Wyndham Vale,
Docklands and Tarneit all evidence it).

Every page therefore answers it affirmatively but **conditionally**: describe the model
first, we confirm it suits the door before anything is booked. No page promises to fit
anything the customer turns up with.

**Ask:** is fit-only on customer-supplied hardware something you do routinely, and are
there brands or types you decline? If there are exclusions, the pages should name them.

## 3. Do you hang or replace doors, or only the hardware?

**Status:** written as NOT offered.

The Flemington research contains a local job requesting a front door replacement
("removal and installation of new door"). Door hanging does not appear in
`client-profile.json` `services`, which lists lock and hardware work only.

The Flemington page therefore states the door itself has to be sorted before the hardware
goes on, **without** claiming Global Locksmiths does that work. Several other suburbs
(Seddon, Tarneit, Werribee) involve doors needing preparation, so the same boundary will
recur.

**Ask:** do you replace or hang doors, or is that referred out? If you do, the pages can
say so and it is a genuine differentiator on the unprepared-door suburbs.

## 4. The live site still advertises claims you have ruled out

**Status:** known and accepted by the client; recorded here so it is not lost.

The existing website and its suburb pages advertise round-the-clock availability,
after-hours callout and a 30-to-60-minute arrival window. All were ruled out at intake:
the offer is Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, with no arrival-time promise. The client has
said this will be corrected at the next website update.

The 41 new pages carry none of these claims. Until the live pages are fixed, the site
contradicts itself, and the Brighton page currently ranking with "Fast 24/7 Mobile" in its
title tag is the most visible instance.

**Ask:** confirm the timing of that correction, so the new pages do not launch alongside
contradicting copy.
