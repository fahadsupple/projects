## Choosing Between Keypad, Fingerprint and App Entry

The brand matters less than most people expect. How you want to open the door on an ordinary Tuesday shapes daily life more.

- Keypad entry: a code instead of a key, handed out and taken back as you need. It suits a household where a cleaner, a tradesperson or adult children come and go.
- Fingerprint entry: nothing to carry, nothing to remember. Prints are enrolled at handover and more can be added later.
- App and WiFi entry: unlock the door when you're not there, and check it locked behind you. It leans on your home network reaching the door.
- A mechanical key override: many smart locks keep a keyed cylinder as a fallback and some do not. Settle that before you buy, because an override cannot be added later.

Most locks combine two or three. Tell us how the household comes and goes and we'll narrow it with you.

## Fitting a Smart Lock to a Lockwood, Lane or Lemaar Door

If your entry door was hung in the last few decades, the lockset on it is likely to carry one of those three names. All of them make both mortice locks and deadbolts, so a retrofit comes down to which type is on your door.

A smart deadbolt going in where a deadbolt already sits is the straightforward version: if the bore diameter, the backset and the door thickness suit the new lock, it uses the existing hole. A smart mortice lock matches the pocket cut into the edge of the door instead. Where the footprints differ, the door is made good so the new plate sits flat.

If the lock keeps a keyed cylinder and you want the rest of the house on that key, raise it at the quote.

## The Doors That Change the Job

Mention it when you enquire if your entry is one of these, because it decides what fits.

- Aluminium-framed doors with glazed panels have a narrow stile, so there is limited width for the lock body and drilling is usually part of the work. Slim smart locks exist for this.
- On an apartment or common-property entrance, a fire-rated door restricts what may be fitted, which is why fire-rated smart locks for unit doors exist. The owners corporation usually has a say too.
- A security screen door carries its own lock, so we work out how the two operate together before either changes.
- Sliding and bifold doors take different hardware again, and not every smart lock has a version for them.
- A door with no bore at all is prepared from scratch, a longer job than a swap but a routine one.

None of this rules a smart lock out. It changes which lock, and it is easier to settle at the quote.

## Questions We Get Before a Smart Lock Goes On

### Do smart locks work without WiFi?

Most do. Keypad and fingerprint entry runs on the lock itself, with no network involved. WiFi adds the remote side: unlocking from your phone while you are out, and seeing that the door locked. If the entry sits far from the router, raise that before choosing a model.

### What happens if the battery goes flat?

Smart locks warn you well before they run down, on the lock and usually in the app. Backups differ by model: some keep the override key, some take a temporary power connection on the outside. Which one applies to yours is among the things we go through at handover.

### What does it cost to have a smart lock installed in Melbourne?

It is quoted per door, free and with no obligation. What moves the number is what is on the door now, what is going on, whether the door needs preparing, and how many doors at once. You get the figure before any work begins.
