## How Much Lock an Aluminium Stile Will Carry

On your door the lock has to live in the vertical section running down the opening edge, called the stile. On a glazed aluminium entry that section is a strip rather than a face.

Put a body built for a solid timber leaf onto a narrow stile and the outer plate can end up sitting over the glazing bead instead of flat on the metal. Slim-profile models exist for that reason, and they are why the shortlist on a door like yours gets shorter rather than longer.

Four things get measured before a model is confirmed:

- The width of the flat metal between the glazing bead and the outside edge of the door. That one figure rules most lock bodies in or out.
- How far in from the edge the lock centres have to sit. A narrow stile leaves very little room to move them.
- The thickness of the door, and what sits behind the frame face, since fixings pass through a hollow section rather than into solid timber.
- What is in the stile now. Where a narrow-stile mortice lock is already there, a smart mortice lock may be able to use the preparation. A plain smart deadbolt, drawn for a timber leaf, rarely suits this door.

A tape measure settles all four in a couple of minutes, with the door hanging where it hangs.

## Drilling Metal Next to a Pane of Glass

If you have been picturing a hole saw going through your front door, that is the timber version of this job.

Metal is cut rather than bored. The openings are stepped up in stages with bits made for aluminium, run slower than timber needs, and the filings caught as they come off rather than left on the sill or the glazing.

The glass sets the working room. Nothing goes through the pane. Every hole is in the metal, and the glazing bead beside it is covered while the cutting happens. Positions come off the lock's own template with the door in its frame, so the bolt and the keeper it shoots into agree with each other.

Cutting is permanent, which is why the model is settled and the frame measured beforehand.

The lock then goes on and is set up with you there: codes loaded, fingerprints enrolled if it reads them, the app paired where there is one. You open and lock the door yourself before the van is packed.

## What People Ask About Aluminium Doors and Slim Locks

### How do I know whether my frame is wide enough for the lock I want?

Measure across the flat metal on the opening edge, from the glazing bead out to the edge of the door, and hold that against the model's own dimensions. That is what the free quote visit is for.

### Does anything get drilled through the glass?

No. All of the cutting is in the metal frame, and the glazing beside it is covered while it happens. A pane is not something a lock is ever fixed to.

### The model I like is not sold as a narrow-stile version. Does that rule it out?

For that opening, usually. Plenty of ranges do make a slim body for exactly this kind of door, so where yours does not, the useful answer is which ones do rather than persuading the wrong one on.

### Is an aluminium door dearer to fit than a timber one?

Often, though the frame is only half of it. Cutting metal is slower and more careful than boring timber, while a stile already carrying a narrow-stile lock can save the preparation altogether. The quote is per door and free either way.

### The lock has been in that frame since the house was built. Does it have to come out?

Not necessarily. Where it is sound and the new lock is going above or below it, it stays, and the door ends up with two locks on it. Where it is worn, or the smart lock wants that exact position, it comes off.
