## Cutting and Drilling a Door That Was Never Prepared

If nothing has ever been cut into your front door, you are not starting from behind. You are starting with a free choice of where the lock sits.

A smart deadbolt expects two openings, and plenty of front doors have neither. One is a round bore through the face, set a measured distance in from the edge. The other is a hole through the edge itself, for the bolt to travel through. A recess in the jamb catches that bolt when the door shuts.

Where none of it exists, it gets made at your address. The door is measured, the centres are marked off the lock's own template, and the bore is cut with a hole saw sized to that model. The edge is drilled square to the face so the bolt runs straight instead of binding. The strike is marked with the door closed, so the bolt finds its recess without anyone leaning on the door. Other front-door hardware goes on the same way here: an entry set with pull handles drilled through both sides and a roller latch cut into the edge.

A solid timber leaf and a metal-framed door do not cut alike, so say which you have when you enquire.

There may already be some of this in your door, which is worth checking before you assume a cut. A bore through the face takes a smart deadbolt directly, provided the bore size, backset and thickness suit the model. Where a mortice lock sits in the edge instead, the pocket routed for it can carry a smart mortice lock. Lockwood, Lane and Lemaar cover much of what is on Melbourne doors, and all three make both types.

Fitting follows the preparation on the same visit. The door gets closed and opened a few times first, to confirm the bolt is finding its recess cleanly. Then your codes go on, fingerprints are enrolled if the lock reads them, and the app is paired where there is one. Nothing is called finished until you have locked and unlocked it yourself.

## A Lock You Bought Yourself Still Needs a Hole

You may have the lock already. That is a normal booking. Hardware you bought yourself gets the same fitting as hardware we bring. If the model is sitting in its box, an Aqara A100 or anything else, the job is the hole and the fitting, and the model sets the dimensions everything is cut to. The make and model is enough to settle fit, so include it in the enquiry rather than saving it for the day.

If you have not bought yet, three things narrow the choice:

- The backset the model is built for, meaning how far the bore's centre sits in from the door edge. With nothing cut yet, you can put it exactly where the lock wants it.
- The range of door thicknesses the model covers. It is printed on the box, and it rules doors in and out.
- Whether it keeps a mechanical key cylinder. That is a purchase decision rather than something a locksmith can add later.

## What to Ask Before the Drill Comes Out

### Will you drill the door, or does it need preparing before you arrive?

Preparing it is part of the job. Marking out, boring the face, cutting the edge and setting the strike all happen on site, and there is nothing you need to do to the door beforehand. A bore already in the right place saves that step; it is not a condition of the booking.

### I already have an Aqara A100 and no hole in the door. Will you fit it?

Yes. Supplying your own lock is ordinary and it changes nothing about the standard of the fit. The model itself sets the dimensions to work to: the backset, the bore size, and the door thicknesses it is built for. Name it when you enquire, and nothing gets cut on a guess.

### Does cutting a door cost more than swapping a lock?

Usually. Preparing a door from nothing is more work than fitting into an opening that already suits the lock, and the quote follows the work rather than a rate card. You have the figure before anything starts.

### The front door is metal-framed rather than timber. Does that change anything?

A timber leaf is bored and mortised. A metal-framed door takes different cutting and different fixings. Either way it gets done, and knowing which one yours is decides what gets loaded on the van.

### Can the gate and the shed be sorted on the same visit?

Yes, if they are mentioned at the booking. Gate locks, shed hardware and garage door locks are ordinary work, and a keypad on the front door does not mean the rest has to be electronic.
