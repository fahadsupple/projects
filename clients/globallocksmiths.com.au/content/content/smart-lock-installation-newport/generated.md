## What Changes When the Door Folds

A bifold entry is not one door. It is two or more narrow leaves hinged to each other, and the leaf carrying the lock has less width to give than a standard front door. That narrowness is the whole question, because a lock body needs a certain width of stile to sit in and a certain depth behind the face. The old mortice lock already sits in the edge of that leaf, in a pocket routed to take it, so a smart mortice lock is the natural direction: it uses space that already exists.

Four things settle it:

- How wide the stile is on the locking leaf, which decides whether a lock case fits at all.
- How big the existing mortice pocket is, and whether the new case matches it or needs it adjusted.
- How thick the leaf is, since hardware built for a solid entry door does not always suit a folding panel.
- What happens as the door folds: the handle and keypad have to clear the next leaf, and the keypad still needs to sit where you can reach it.

The same reading applies to any entry that is not a plain hinged leaf. Fewer ranges make something for these doors, so the honest answer is sometimes that the model you liked is not the one, and the reply names what would suit instead.

## Questions About Folding Doors and Choosing a Lock

### Can a smart lock replace the mortice lock on a bifold door?

Often yes. A smart mortice lock takes over the pocket cut into the edge of a door, which is exactly where the old one sits. What gets confirmed is that the leaf is wide enough and thick enough for the new case, and that the pocket matches it or can be adjusted to.

### When the door folds back, where does the keypad end up?

Somewhere you can reach with the door shut, and clear of the next leaf when it is folded open. Both get checked before a model is settled on, because the wrong body depth turns a daily action into a fiddle.

### Is fitting one to a folding door something I could do myself?

A plain hinged door with a deadbolt already in the right place is a job a confident DIYer can manage. A folding leaf is a different proposition: less material to play with, a pocket cut to somebody else's dimensions, and no undoing a cut in the wrong place.

### Which smart lock is best in Australia?

There is no single answer, because it depends on the door before it depends on the brand. The shortlist comes from what your door will physically take, then from how you want to open it and whether you want a mechanical key as a fallback. On a bifold that first filter does most of the narrowing.

### Can you sort a lockable handle for the bathroom at the same visit?

Yes. Internal door hardware is ordinary work, and it goes on the same booking as the front door if you mention it when you enquire. It lands in the same quote too.
