WHAT IS NOT AN ISSUE:
- "program" (not "programme") — In Australian English, "program" is the accepted
  spelling for educational and government programs (e.g. ACECQA uses "program").
  "Programme" is only used for arts/entertainment events. No changes needed.
- "waitlist" — Accepted spelling in Australian English. No changes needed.
- "enrolled" / "enrolling" — These are the same in AU and US English (past
  tense / gerund of "enrol" both use double-L). No changes needed.
- Footer references to other suburbs (Bull Creek on NSW pages, Oakville on WA
  pages) — These are the global site footer listing all Flourish ELC centre
  locations. Expected and correct.
