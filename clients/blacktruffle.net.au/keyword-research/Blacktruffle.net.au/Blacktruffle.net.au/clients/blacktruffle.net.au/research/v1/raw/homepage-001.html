Ordering online?

  1. If you do not already have an account with login details, you will need to create one under your name or the business name, please follow the prompts.
    Create a New Account
  2. Click “Browse menu/Order online”.
  3. Select which food category you want to choose items from down the left-hand side.
  4. Select your items and quantities of each and add to your cart.
  5. Go to check out and finalise your order with all your details. The system will send our team a notification of your order, we will go over it and send a confirmation email back to you stating the order has now been processed.

Please note if you do not receive an electronic confirmation email back, the order may have not been processed properly.

Email Us
You can place your orders via email at catering@blacktruffle.net.au. Make sure to include all the details such as date, time, location and how many people you want to cater for.

Call us
Our friendly office team can be contacted on 03 9419 9290 to place orders over the phone.

During COVID 19

Food and safety is always paramount at Black Truffle Catering. With stricter food safety rules now in place with the emergence of Covid19, Black Truffle Catering adhere to the guidelines provided by the Food Standards Australia New Zealand and regulatory government authorities.

  • On-going safety and training of employees
  • All staff wear protective gear (facemasks and gloves)
  • Practice safe hygiene with washing / sanitizing of hands
  • With dedicated food delivery vehicles Black Truffle Catering provides a contactless delivery service
