Strategic Narratives

5 critical finding(s) for this run.

The suburb grid is a deliberate zero-volume land grab, not a data failure

32 of the client's 39 declared suburbs return zero recorded search volume, and 29 of the 36 selected grid keywords are zero even at national scope. This is the correct strategy for this market, not a gap in the research: the recovered Adelaide Detailing Garage portfolio shows 16 of 20 suburb pages holding position 1 on exactly these zero-volume terms. Suburb pages here win local-intent and map-pack traffic that keyword tools do not record. Any downstream filter that drops zero-volume keywords would delete 29 of the 36 location deliverables.

Campbelltown volume is inflated 72x by interstate contamination

metrics.json was collected at AU-national scope (location_code 2036), where 'window tinting campbelltown' reads 720/mo and 'ceramic coating campbelltown' 110/mo. Adelaide-scoped probing (location_code 1000422) returns 10/mo for both — the national figure is Campbelltown NSW, a major Sydney centre 1,300km from the Klemzig workshop. Campbelltown SA remains correctly selected on ADG position-1 proof, Q17 naming and inner-east proximity, but forecasts must use 20/mo, not 830/mo. Headline reach of 3,730/mo therefore overstates by ~820/mo; the honest Adelaide-scoped figure is ~2,910/mo. Every other suburb in the grid is consistent across both scopes.

Local pack on 74% of SERPs while the client has no review base

Phase 3c found a local pack present on 31 of 42 probed SERPs. The client has no Google rating and no review count in the questionnaire, and the brand probe returned rank_position absent with 0% SERP control. For a DA-0 domain the map pack is the fastest realistic route to visibility, which makes the GMB deliverable in the BDM brief higher-leverage than the organic content in the first 90 days. The unresolved NAP conflict (questionnaire Q8 says 9am open, the live site says 8am) must be settled before citation building starts.

Weak-authority market — head terms are contestable, contrary to the initial read

Phase 3b measured competitor Domain Authority at 15, 13, 12, 10, 3, 2 and 1 — no rival exceeds DA 15. Head-term difficulty is correspondingly low: 'paint protection adelaide' and 'ceramic coating adelaide' both KD 0, 'window tinting adelaide' KD 3, 'ppf adelaide' KD 7. A Facebook page currently holds position 3 for 'paint protection adelaide' (880/mo, $5.71 CPC). The Phase 0 assumption that head terms were a 12-month play against entrenched incumbents was written before DA data existed and has been corrected in allocation.json.

SERP probe coverage is partial

Phase 3c probed 42 of 166 candidates at --per-silo-n 14 (reduced from 20 after the first 60-probe run was interrupted by a session restart at probe 23). All 4 general keywords and the highest-value location terms were probed; lower-ranked zero-volume suburb cells were not. No selected keyword relies on an unprobed SERP for its reasoning.
